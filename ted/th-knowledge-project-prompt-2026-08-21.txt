=== 1. WHAT TED'S HEALTH IS ===

Ted's Health is Bear Grylls Ventures' (BGV) UK men's-health telehealth brand — TRT (testosterone replacement therapy), ED (erectile dysfunction) treatment, hormone optimisation, blood testing, and now weight-loss medication, delivered through a regulated, CQC-registered UK clinical pipeline. Patients get prescriber consultations, blood testing, and ongoing prescriptions through TH's clinical and pharmacy infrastructure.

=== 2. WHAT THE WHITE-LABEL (WL) PARTNERSHIP IS ===

WL lets another business (a clinic, gym, aesthetics practice, wellness brand, or individual operator) offer TH's clinical services under their own brand, without building any clinical capability themselves.

What the partner brings: their existing patient/client base, their brand, and (where relevant) their physical space.
What Ted's Health brings: the CQC-registered clinical team, prescribers, pharmacy fulfilment, compliance, and the full patient pathway (consultation, prescriber review, blood testing, prescription, delivery, repeat scripts).

The partner does not need to register as a clinic, hire prescribers, or handle any regulatory burden — that risk and infrastructure sits entirely with Ted's Health. The partner is effectively adding a clinical revenue line on top of a relationship they already have.

=== 3. HOW REVENUE SPLITS WORK ===

Two different models depending on the item:

TIER 1 — Medication (TRT, tadalafil/ED, and the GLP-1 weight-loss range):
Partner buys each item from Ted's Health at a wholesale price (TH's cost plus a 37% uplift already built in). The partner then sets their own patient-facing price with no restriction, and keeps 100% of everything above that wholesale price. TH's income is fixed at the point the partner buys the medication — it does not depend on what the partner charges the patient.

TIER 2 — Services (consultations, blood tests, phlebotomy, consumables):
Supplied to the partner at cost, no markup from TH. Whatever price the partner sets, they keep 63% of the markup and TH takes 37%. If the partner chooses to sell at cost, they owe TH nothing beyond that cost.

In short: on medication, the split is baked into the wholesale price and the partner has full pricing freedom above it. On services, it's a straight 63/37 split (partner/TH) on whatever markup is added.
