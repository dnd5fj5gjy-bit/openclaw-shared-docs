TH White-Label — Refined Outreach Target List

Platforms that would plug in TH's white-label, not build it themselves

Refined from the first target list: this set is screened for platforms that currently lack TRT prescribing AND blood testing/diagnostics (not just GLP-1), and for which building CQC-registered clinical infrastructure in-house would be a genuine lift they'd rather avoid.

Every candidate below was checked against TH's full service set: TRT, ED treatment, GLP-1/weight-loss, and blood testing/diagnostics. Companies confirmed to already run any of this in-house are excluded and listed at the bottom so they don't get re-pitched. Verified using live web search, current as of 28 August 2026.

Strongest four fits: Doctor Care Anywhere, Champion Health, Levels Health, ExSeed Health — confirmed appetite for adjacent clinical modules, no in-house CQC build.

Digital men's health apps and platforms

ExSeed Health
At-home fertility/sperm testing, 60,000+ users, CE-marked
Sperm testing only, no hormone bloodwork, no TRT/ED/weight-loss prescribing. Their fertility-concerned users are prime TRT/hormone-panel candidates already. Building CQC prescribing is a big lift for a niche fertility-test company.
Legion Athletics (Muscle For Life)
US supplement/fitness-media brand, large male audience
Constant testosterone content, but founder publicly refuses to sell testosterone-booster supplements he doesn't believe work — an explicit "buy don't build" signal. Could genuinely refer or white-label a real TRT pathway to an audience already primed for the topic.

Fitness, longevity and biohacking platforms

Levels Health
CGM/metabolic-health app
Publishes detailed TRT/hormone-testing guides but does not prescribe medication or hormones themselves. Content-market-fit for TRT is obvious; a second regulated pathway makes no sense for a metabolic-data company to build.
Ultrahuman
Wearable ring + "Blood Vision" biomarker panel
No prescribing capability at all. A consumer hardware/data company, not a clinical operator.
InsideTracker
Biomarker-testing membership, includes a testosterone test on the Ultimate plan
Does not diagnose, treat or prescribe — a physician only authorises the lab draw. Partial overlap on blood testing, but the treatment layer is a clean gap.
Whoop
Wearable, new "Advanced Labs" blood-testing tie-up with Quest Diagnostics
No TRT/hormone prescribing. Blood testing is no longer a clean gap given the Quest partnership, but the prescribing module (TRT/ED/GLP-1) is entirely absent.

Smaller/mid-size telehealth platforms without a men's health vertical

Doctor Care Anywhere
UK's largest private telehealth GP provider, LSE-listed, 2.3M patients
Just acquired a weight-management platform for £0.9M rather than building one in-house — direct evidence they prefer plug-in/M&A over build for adjacent clinical verticals. No TRT/men's-health offering yet. One of the strongest fits on the list.
Livi
UK's biggest video-GP platform, CQC Outstanding, 11M+ patients across Europe
Purely general practice, no dedicated TRT/men's-health/weight-loss vertical. Already CQC-familiar and used to regulated telehealth — strong "plug in a ready module" logic.
Champion Health
UK workplace wellbeing platform, part of Physitrack
MSK, mental health, general health assessments — no clinical prescribing, no men's-health vertical. Actively expanding "holistic care" post-acquisition; a module partnership fits their direction better than building regulated prescribing from scratch.

Corporate wellness tech, mid-size B2B

Peppy Health
B2B employee-benefit app, clinician-led menopause/fertility/men's health services
Already prescribes on the menopause side (MHT). Men's health service is advice/guidance only, no TRT. They've proven the "clinical service via app benefit" model already — a TRT/blood-test module is a logical licence-in rather than a second regulated pathway to build.
Juno
UK flexible-benefits/wellbeing marketplace, 10,000+ products/services
A marketplace aggregator with no clinical delivery capability of its own. Would add TH as a marketplace partner rather than build.
Wellhub (formerly Gympass)
Global fitness-and-wellbeing benefits subscription
Fitness, mindfulness, nutrition, sleep — no clinical/prescribing layer. Larger scale than the others here, but still a benefits-aggregation model, not a clinical operator.

Grooming, subscription and lifestyle brands with a loyal male base

Harry's
Grooming subscription brand, large male subscriber base, existing subscription-commerce infrastructure
No health services currently. Extending into TRT/ED/weight-loss as a white-label add-on to an existing subscription relationship is plausible, but speculative — no direct evidence they're considering clinical services. Included as illustrative of this category.
