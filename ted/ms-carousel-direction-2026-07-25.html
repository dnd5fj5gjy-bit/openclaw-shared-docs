01 / The content stays

Keep it, and keep varying it

The hooks, titles and themes are landing - do not change them. "The number one supplement ingredient... but everybody ignores it," "the most nourishing parts of your food were quietly removed" - this is exactly the tone we want.

Keep the setup-then-italic-payoff device, keep the brand marks, and keep producing in these same themes with fresh variations each time. Same voice, new angles.

02 / The aesthetic

Real, empowering, premium

01

Real, not stock-y

Editorial imagery with natural light and shallow depth of field - it should look shot, not sourced. If it feels like a catalogue photo, it is not there yet.

02

Real greenery, alive

Push into deep, natural, sun-touched greens and warm earth tones. Lush and golden-hour, never flat olive or muddy.

03

Empowering framing

Low angles, strong silhouettes, the raw power of the animal and the landscape. Every slide should make the viewer feel strength.

04

Let it breathe

One clear subject, strong negative space, and room around the type. Crowded reads cheap; space reads premium.

05

Texture and craft

Natural materials - stone, hide, paper, soil, grain. Tactility is what separates premium from generic.

06

One consistent grade

Every slide graded the same way, same type scale, same margins - so the whole set feels designed and engaging as a piece.

Keep every hook, title and theme - just keep varying them. Lift the aesthetic to real, empowering and premium: deep natural greenery, golden-hour light, strong framing, one consistent grade and an editorial serif across the set. Same message, far more premium and engaging.
