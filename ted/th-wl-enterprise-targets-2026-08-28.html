TH White-Label — Outreach Target List

Enterprise-scale companies for the TH White-Label service

Targets much larger than TH's current partner base (independent clinics and gyms), where TH's white-label GLP-1 and weight-loss service could sit inside an existing large customer, member, or employee base — not as a referral, but as their own branded offering.

Every candidate below was checked for whether it already runs a GLP-1 or medical weight-loss offering. Companies with a confirmed existing arrangement are listed separately and marked as low priority, since the opportunity there is to displace an incumbent rather than fill a gap. Confirmed as of 28 August 2026 using live web search; some entries need a direct verification call before an outreach email goes out, flagged where relevant.

Best six to lead with today: PureGym, The Gym Group, David Lloyd Clubs, AXA Health, Aviva, Simplyhealth. Largest bases, cleanest confirmation of no existing GLP-1 offering.

Gaps and notes

Direct employers. No named UK case studies found of large direct employers — logistics, retail, manufacturing — offering GLP-1 as an employee benefit. This looks like genuine unclaimed territory but needs company-by-company outreach rather than a pre-vetted list. Treat it as a prospecting lane, not a confirmed target set.
International reference points. Hims & Hers and Omada Health both run large-scale GLP-1 telehealth/employer programmes in the US already. Not White-Label candidates themselves, but useful as competitive-positioning reference in outreach decks.
