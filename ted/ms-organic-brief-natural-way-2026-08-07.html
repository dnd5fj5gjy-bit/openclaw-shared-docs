01

The idea

This is the one post in the mix with nothing to prove and nothing to sell. Not a comparison, not a point being made, just the scale and beauty of the planet on its own, scored to lift. It exists to make someone stop scrolling and feel grounded for fifteen seconds. The grounding lives in the caption, never in the footage, so we let the image simply be beautiful.

The one rule everything hangs on

No text on screen. The frame stays pure. Every word lives in the caption.

02

Shot spec

One idea per clip. Let the image breathe. Nothing busy, nothing that needs explaining.

Motion

One continuous shot, no cuts. Slow, unbroken movement: a drone push or pull, an aerial glide, or a slow gimbal move.

15 to 30 seconds. Loop-friendly where the shot allows.

Subject

Pure natural beauty at scale, nothing more: mountain ranges, coastlines, forest canopy, open plains, big weather, rivers, herds.

One subject per clip, no montage. No message, no contrast, no human element unless it reads purely as scale.

Grade & frame

Natural and filmic. Rich but real, not a screensaver. No logo bug, no graphics.

9:16 primary. Frame a 4:5 safe zone from the start so a winner can move to the grid clean (see mechanic).

Music

Empowering and cinematic, a slow build. Orchestral swell or ambient pads that rise and open out. Reverent and vast, never hype.

Nothing lyrical that competes with the calm. Cleared for commercial use.

04

Reels first, grid on merit

These post as Reels by default. The main grid stays selective and earns its content: only the clips the audience votes for graduate to it, so the storefront stays high-quality.

Step 01

Post to Reels

Run these at volume as Reels only. They're low-risk test units. The looser, hook-led caption can live here.

Step 02

Let the audience vote

Judge on saves and shares, not view count. A save on a nature clip means someone wanted to feel it again, which is exactly the effect we're after.

Step 03

Winners earn the grid

Graduate standouts to the main grid, re-cropped to the 4:5 safe zone, and swap in the cleaner tagline-led caption for a more brand-aware audience.

To confirm: set a real bar for "winner" so it isn't a judgment call each time, for example top performer per batch, or a save / share threshold. Tell me your number and I'll write it into the brief.
05

Guardrails

Please hold these.

  1. Nothing on screen. No burned-in text, no captions in-frame, no logo bug, no graphics. If a clip needs a word on screen to make sense, it belongs in a different pillar.
  2. No sell in the video. The clip is pure nature and scale. Modern Savage enters only in the written caption under the post.
  3. No health claims. Grounding and reconnection framing only. Nothing that says the product boosts, treats, fixes or prevents anything.
  4. Music must be cleared for commercial use before it goes near a post.
