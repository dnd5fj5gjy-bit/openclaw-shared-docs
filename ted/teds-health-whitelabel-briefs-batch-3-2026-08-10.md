Category: Social media graphics
Task Name: TH WL — First 90 Days

Slide 2: "Week 1: Strategy call with our team, including our Medical Director. We work out which partnership structure fits your business."

Slide 3: "Weeks 2-3: Self-serve setup — your branding, your subdomain, your admin account. No development team required on your side."

Slide 4 — CTA: "From there: your platform is live, and your first patient is a conversation away. Book a partner strategy call — tedshealth.com/partner-with-us"

Footer (slide 4): "CQC-registered. GMC doctors. Live prescribing already in place."
Contact: "partnerships@tedshealth.com"

Tone: Plain, operational, reassuring. The point is to kill the "this will take 12 months and a dev team" assumption partners bring in from other categories (software integrations, clinical build-outs).

Visual guidance: Clean numbered carousel, navy base with warm gold accent, each slide anchored by a small supporting photo (a call in progress, a laptop showing branding setup, a phone showing the live branded platform). No stock clock/calendar icon clichés — let the copy and week numbers carry the pacing. Stock search terms: "video call laptop office," "branding setup screen laptop hands," "phone showing branded app screen."
