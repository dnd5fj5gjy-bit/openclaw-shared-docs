How a partner uses these. The partner receives each email as finished, partner-branded HTML, with their name, address, tracked link and (optionally) live price filled in. They send it to their own contacts who have opted in to marketing.

01 · Rules for all seven

Built into every email

Always

  • Promote the test, the consultation and the process
  • Say that a UK-registered clinician reviews every case
  • Say treatment is only offered where clinically appropriate, and it is the clinician's decision
  • Adults aged 18 and over
  • Provider line in the footer
  • Opt-out link and consent line in the footer
  • Clinical questions go to support@tedshealth.com

Never

  • Medicine names, brands, pens, needles, tablets, gels
  • Results, outcomes, timeframes or before and after
  • Offers, discount codes or free trials
  • Choosing recipients from health records
  • Saying the partner prescribes or provides clinical care
  • Ted's Health as the sender or brand
Why 'TRT' is not in the copy. Promoting testosterone treatment is promoting a prescription-only medicine, and a controlled drug. The emails promote the men's hormone health check instead. The label 'TRT' is still with the professional advisers, so it stays out of subject lines and body copy. Email 2 and email 5 are the TRT emails internally.

Fields the partner fills in: [Clinic name], [First name], [Address], collection routes offered, tracked link, privacy notice link, and (optional) one live service-level price.
