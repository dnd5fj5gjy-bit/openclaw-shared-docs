The case

Building it yourself, against building it with us.

Both routes get you to a men’s health offer. They differ on how long it takes, what it costs you, and who carries the risk.

Building your own clinical service

  • Register with the CQC as a healthcare provider and pass inspection before you can trade
  • Recruit and indemnify prescribing clinicians, and stand up a clinical governance framework
  • Contract a registered dispensing pharmacy and a diagnostics and phlebotomy network
  • Commission a compliant patient platform covering intake, consultations, prescriptions and results
  • Carry full clinical liability for every decision made under your name
  • Twelve months and a substantial capital outlay before the first patient pays you anything

White-label with Ted’s Health

  • Our clinicians, our pharmacy partner, our diagnostics network, already contracted and running
  • A built platform, branded to you, live in weeks rather than quarters
  • Clinical liability sits with us. Your liability covers your own marketing and your own business
  • You set your own retail prices
  • Low cost to start, and revenue from the customer base you already own
Commercials

How the money works.

There are two ways the money splits, depending on what is being sold.

Medication

We charge you a wholesale cost. You set your retail price. Everything above our wholesale cost is yours. We do not take a percentage of it.

Everything else

Consultations, blood tests, phlebotomy, consumables. We take our cost off the top, then the profit above that is split 63% to you, 37% to us.

So we take the wholesale cost plus 37% of the margin, and you keep the remaining 63%.

Getting paid

It all happens automatically at the point the customer pays. The payment is split at checkout, so your share lands in your account and ours lands in ours. No invoicing back and forth, no chasing.

  • Your share goes into your own Ryft sub-account, and you are the merchant of record for the sale to your customer.
  • A £500 onboarding deposit is payable when you join, through the Payments settings page in the platform. It is earned back automatically out of our share of your early sales, and the split returns to normal by itself once it clears. It shows as its own line on your monthly statement.
  • A monthly statement sets out every transaction, the split applied and the VAT treatment of each line.
  • We will need your VAT registration status and number when you sign, because it changes how your commission is treated.

Our wholesale costs sit behind the platform and are treated as confidential. The figures above apply across every service line switched on for you.

Getting started

From yes to live.

The sequence below is the whole process. Most of it is admin on our side once you have signed.

  1. Agreement signedWe send the white-label agreement along with an Order Form confirming which service lines you want switched on. At the same time we take your legal entity name, company number, registered address, VAT registration status and number, and evidence of your insurance.
  2. Platform set-upYou complete Ryft onboarding, which creates the sub-account your share of every sale is paid into, then run through the platform setup wizard. We apply your branding and issue logins to whoever on your team needs them.
  3. DepositOnce you are set up, a card appears on your Payments settings page to pay the £500 deposit. It is earned back automatically out of our share of your early sales and the split returns to normal by itself once it clears.
  4. Go liveYour offer opens to your customers. We handle every patient from that point forward, we will help you with the launch copy if it is useful, and you get a monthly statement covering everything that traded.
