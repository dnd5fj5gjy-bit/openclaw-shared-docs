01 · In short

The patient pays once. The payment splits automatically.

You are the seller. Patients buy from your clinic, at the price you set, through your Ted's Health platform. The payment is split at source, so you are not waiting for someone to send you a transfer and you do not have to chase an invoice for your share of a sale.

1

You connect Ryft

You set up a Ryft account and complete its identity checks (KYC and KYB). This is step 5 of your online sign-up.

2

Patient pays

The patient pays your retail price at your checkout, in one payment.

3

Automatic split

Ryft divides the payment between your account and Ted's Health, following the pricing model for that item.

4

Fees come off

Ryft deducts its processing fees and the split-payment fee.

5

You receive your share

The net amount reaches your account. Payout timing: to confirm with Ryft

You are not paid per patient referred. Your income is your share of what patients pay for the services you sell. We do not pay a fee for each patient you send, and this is deliberate.
02 · Two pricing models

Medication and services work differently

There is no single blanket percentage. Which model applies depends on what the patient is buying. Your exact figures are in your agreement and your Order Form.

Prescription medication

  • Ted's Health notifies you of a Wholesale Cost for each treatment. It is confidential to you.
  • You set your own retail price.
  • Ted's Health receives the Wholesale Cost. You keep everything above it, after payment fees.
  • There is no percentage split on medication.

Consultations and services

  • Covers consultations, blood tests, phlebotomy appointments, consumables and other designated services.
  • You set your own retail price.
  • Ted's Health receives the cost of providing the service, plus an agreed share of the margin. You receive the rest.
  • The margin is your price minus that cost. The share applies to the margin only, never to the full sale value.
Your price is your decision. Ted's Health may publish recommended prices, but they are guidance only. You can ignore them. If a price you set falls below the Wholesale Cost, your agreement allows us to invoice the difference, payable within 30 days (clause 8.6). Please check this clause with your adviser if you plan to price low.

Wholesale Costs and platform fees can change on written notice where supplier, pharmacy or laboratory costs, regulation or tax change (clause 8.11).

03 · What you will see

Why two numbers will never match

You will see your sales in two places. They are meant to differ.

WhereWhat it showsWhy it is different
Your platform dashboardWhat patients ordered, at your full retail price. This is the gross figure.It is the value of the sale before anything is split or deducted.
Your Ryft accountWhat you actually received. This is the net figure.It is after Ted's Health's share, Ryft's processing fees and the split-payment fee.

If the two totals differ, that is the model working as designed, not an error. You can reconcile from one to the other: gross sales, less the split and fees, equals the net you received.

Monthly report: format and delivery date to be confirmed. Ryft fee rates: to be confirmed with Ryft and your Order Form.

04 · Invoices and VAT

Three documents for every sale

Because you sell in your own name while Ted's Health provides the clinical service, each sale produces three invoices:

#From and toWhat it is
1You to the patientThe full sale value. Ted's Health does not appear on it.
2Ted's Health to youThe same supplies at the same values as invoice 1.
3You to Ted's HealthYour commission, self-billed: Ted's Health raises it on your behalf, under a self-billing agreement that you sign.

The platform generates these from the same sale data so they always agree.

What we need from you before commission invoices can be issued

  • Your registered business address
  • Your VAT registration number, if you are VAT-registered. We verify it and record the date we checked.
  • A signed self-billing agreement on file
  • Completed Ryft identity checks (KYC and KYB)
This is not tax advice. How VAT applies to your commission depends on your own circumstances. Please speak to your accountant. Invoice queries go to invoices@tedshealth.com.
05 · Refunds and chargebacks

Refunds are handled by Ted's Health

Refunds

  • Only Ted's Health staff can issue a refund. Partners cannot refund patients directly (clause 8.9).
  • Where a payment was split, the refund is shared proportionally, and any share already paid out is adjusted.
  • If a patient asks you for a refund, send the request to partner support contact.

Chargebacks

  • If a patient disputes a payment with their bank, tell us promptly (clause 8.7).
  • Where a chargeback results from misinformation, an unauthorised refund, a breach or fraud on the partner's side, the partner is responsible for it under the agreement.
06 · Your set-up list

Get paid on day one

  • Ryft account connected and identity checks (KYC and KYB) completed
  • Payout bank details entered and verified
  • Registered business address held on file
  • VAT registration number provided, if registered
  • Self-billing agreement signed
  • Your retail prices set in the platform for each service you offer
  • You know who to contact: invoices@tedshealth.com for invoices and charges, support@tedshealth.com for any clinical question
A failed Ryft check can lead to suspension. If your identity checks are not completed, payments cannot be processed (clause 17.1). Please complete them as early as you can.
07 · Questions we hear most

Quick answers

Why does my Ryft total not match my platform sales?

The platform shows the full retail value. Ryft shows what reached you after the split and fees. See section 03.

When do I get paid?

Your share is paid into your Ryft account automatically when the patient pays. Time to your bank follows your Ryft payout schedule: to confirm.

Do I have to charge the recommended price?

No. Recommended prices are guidance only. You set your own prices.

Do I earn something for each patient I send?

No. You earn your share of what patients pay for the services you sell. We do not pay per referral.

Can I refund a patient myself?

No. Refunds are processed by Ted's Health staff. Send us the request and we will deal with it.

Where can I see the exact figures for my clinic?

In your agreement and Order Form. Wholesale Costs are confidential to you and are notified separately.
