01 · Why this matters

Prescription-only medicines cannot be advertised to the public

This is the law in the UK, not a Ted's Health preference. Advertising a prescription-only medicine to the public is prohibited under the Human Medicines Regulations 2012, and the advertising code (CAP Code, rule 12.12) says the same. The advertising regulator and the medicines regulator both act on this, and weight loss, testosterone and erectile dysfunction adverts are watched closely.

It is easy to break by accident: a product name in a hashtag, a pen in a photo, an alt-text description. The good news is that you can promote everything that matters to your patients, the test, the consultation, the privacy and the convenience, without ever naming a medicine.

What is at stake for you. Your agreement with Ted's Health sets out the marketing rules (clauses 7.2 to 7.5). Misleading advertising and repeated breaches of the brand guidelines are grounds for suspension of your account (clause 17.1). Following this guide keeps you well inside the lines.
02 · The one rule

Promote the service. Never the medicine.

If you remember one thing, remember this. Talk about what the patient does, not what they might be prescribed.

Talk about

  • The blood test
  • The online consultation or eligibility check
  • Review by a UK-registered clinician
  • Privacy, convenience and access
  • How the process works, step by step

Never mention

  • Any medicine name or brand, or its active ingredient
  • Pens, needles, vials, tablets or gels, in words or pictures
  • Results, before and after images, or time-bound outcomes
  • Offers or discounts tied to a treatment

04 · By service

What you can say about each service

Blood testing Ready

Promote: the range of panels, the Testosterone Blood Test and Wellman add-ons, and the ways to collect your sample that your clinic offers.

Avoid: claiming a test detects more than it does ("cancer screening", "full body scan"), fear-led messages, and turnaround or accuracy promises.

Men's hormone health Ready

Promote: a testosterone blood test plus a review by a clinician, for adult men who want to check how they are doing.

Avoid: suggesting that tiredness or low mood means low testosterone, gym or physique imagery, and naming any treatment. Please use "men's hormone health" rather than "TRT" in public material until we confirm the final label.

Erectile dysfunction Depends on your setup

Promote: a private, confidential online consultation reviewed by a clinician. Public promotion is for clinics where patients can reach the service directly. If your clinic refers patients in person, use the in-clinic referral material only.

Avoid: tablets, performance or "last longer" claims and innuendo.

Weight management Only when released to you

Weight management material is released to a clinic only when your contract is signed, the service is switched on for your clinic, and the pack has been approved. Do not promote it before then.

Avoid: any medicine name, the words "jab" or "injection", pen images, weight lost in a set time, and before and after photos.

06 · Who provides care

Your brand sells. Ted's Health provides the clinical care.

Patients see your clinic's brand. The clinical assessment, prescribing and blood test review are provided by Ted's Health (GSET Health Ltd), a CQC-registered provider. Patients must be told who provides the clinical service, so a short line is used on your web pages, leaflets and email footers:

Provider line (wording being confirmed):
"Clinical assessment, prescribing and blood test review are provided by Ted's Health (GSET Health Ltd), a CQC-registered provider." final wording to be confirmed
  • Do not use Ted's Health logos or marks without written consent (clause 7.2).
  • Do not present Ted's Health as the patient-facing brand (clause 6.5).
  • The CQC statement belongs to Ted's Health as the provider. Do not say your own clinic is CQC-registered for these services unless it separately is.

08 · Before you post

The 10-second check

  • No medicine name, brand, pen, needle, vial or tablet anywhere, including hashtags and alt text
  • No results, guarantees, timeframes or before and after images
  • Written for adults aged 18 and over
  • Wording comes from your pack, or has an approval code
  • Provider line is included where needed
  • Any price matches your live checkout price today
  • No discount or offer tied to a treatment
  • Email and SMS go only to contacts who opted in, with an opt-out
  • Weight management only if it has been released to your clinic
  • Clinical questions go to support@tedshealth.com
