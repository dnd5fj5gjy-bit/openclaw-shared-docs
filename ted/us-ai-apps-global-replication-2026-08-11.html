The Model

Find proven US consumer AI products, replicate the mechanic, launch localized versions in markets the US player hasn't seriously entered.

The Filter

Every app below is scored on real traction (revenue, downloads, funding) then on how buildable a competitor actually is.

The Output

A ranked shortlist of five opportunities plus one category flagged for brand-fit risk before anything gets greenlit.

The strongest candidates aren't the biggest names. ChatGPT and Perplexity are near-impossible to clone — frontier model cost and index scale make them a dead end for a challenger. The real openings are in categories where the US winner has a proven mechanic but hasn't localized the training data, pricing, or content for a specific region — food photography for South Asian cuisine, product photos for Southeast Asian marketplace sellers, companion chat for MENA. That gap is buildable in months, not years.
Ranked Shortlist

Where to move first

Ordered by evidence of US demand weighed against how realistic the build and localization actually is.

01

Food/calorie tracker for South Asian, MENA & Latin American cuisine

Cal AI went $0→$40-50M ARR in 18 months on one insight: photo beats manual logging. Its food-recognition model is Western-trained and weak on dal, biryani, shawarma, home-cooked Brazilian dishes. Low tech barrier, clear data gap, proven category economics.

02

Product-photo tool for Southeast Asian & Latin American social-commerce sellers

PhotoRoom is at $150M+ ARR serving Etsy/Shopify sellers. Shopee, Tokopedia and Mercado Livre seller bases are booming and underserved by anything this polished. Background-removal AI is commodity tech — the win is UX and marketplace-specific integration.

03

Companion/character AI for MENA or South Asia

Zeta (Korean, Scatter Lab) proved the exact playbook — fast-followed the US companion category, localized hard into Japan, and is now the category's #1 grosser there. MENA/South Asia are conspicuously unlocalized. Highest revenue-per-dollar category on this list, but see brand-fit caution below.

04

Photo-enhancer with region-native pricing and payments

Remini already has huge download volume in India but monetizes far below US ARPU. This isn't a build-from-zero opportunity — it's a "beat the incumbent on local pricing and payment rails" opportunity, which is a different and arguably easier motion.

05

Meeting-transcription tuned for outsourcing-hub English accents

Otter/Granola are built and tested on US-accented English. India and the Philippines run huge remote/outsourced workforces where transcription accuracy on local accents is measurably weaker. Less flashy, B2B2C-leaning, but a real and durable niche.

Flag before anything else: brand fit

Companion/romantic AI apps (Zeta, Tipsy Chat, Character.AI-adjacent) are the highest-grossing category per dollar of effort in this entire list — but they're also the category most exposed to App Store policy crackdowns and negative press. Worth serious thought on whether that sits comfortably under a Bear Grylls-adjacent portfolio brand before pursuing #3 above.

Full Research

The 14 apps, one by one

Each entry: what it is, why it's working in the US, and the specific market it's most ready to be replicated into.

1. ChatGPT — OpenAI

Reference / Not a target

General-purpose AI assistant — text, voice, image, agentic tasks, now with shopping checkout built in.

Traction
Fastest app ever to 1B monthly active users (May 2026). ~917M downloads, ~$1.1-1.5B revenue.
Business model
Freemium, $20/mo Plus, plus 4% merchant transaction fee on Instant Checkout.
Barrier to replicate
Very high — compute cost, brand dominance, network effects. Skip as a clone target.
Included for context only — OpenAI is aggressively localizing itself. Not a realistic fast-follow.

2. Character.AI

Companion / Chat

Create and chat with custom AI personas — companions, fictional characters, roleplay.

Traction
~20M MAU (2026). Revenue ~$32-50M (2025), projected ~$60M 2026, +66% YoY.
Business model
Subscription (c.ai+), ads (Feb 2026), usage-metered "Charms" (Mar 2026).
Unlock
First mainstream "talk to any character" product; massive Gen Z persona library.
Barrier to replicate
Moderate — core tech is near-commodity, but the UGC library took years to build.
Best next market: Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Philippines) and Latin America (Brazil, Mexico) — young, mobile-first, but need local-language persona libraries and culturally relevant IP the global platform under-serves.

3. Cal AI

Health / Fitness

Snap a photo of food — AI estimates calories and macros instantly.

Traction
Two teenage founders, bootstrapped $0→$40-50M ARR in 18 months. 15-30M downloads. Acquired by MyFitnessPal, March 2026.
Business model
Freemium, weekly/annual subscription ($6.99-9.99/wk range).
Unlock
One photo replaces MyFitnessPal's 2-minute manual search-and-log. Viral TikTok/Instagram growth loop.
Barrier to replicate
Low-moderate — the tech is replicable within months; the moat is food-image training data and paid-acquisition efficiency.
Best next market: India and MENA — huge diet-conscious populations, but Western-trained food AI performs poorly on dal, biryani, shawarma, mezze. Latin America (Brazil) for the same reason.

4. Remini

Photo / Avatar

Old/blurry photo restoration and AI portrait/avatar generation ("AI Baby," fantasy avatars).

Traction
~$5M/month US App Store revenue (Mar 2026). 7M downloads in one month on Play Store (Jan 2026), lower per-user monetization outside premium markets.
Business model
Freemium, weekly subscription ($6.99-9.99/wk) or $299 annual.
Unlock
Rides viral trend cycles for free acquisition, converts via aggressive subscription paywalls.
Barrier to replicate
Low — one of the most replicable products here; differentiation is UX polish and paywall psychology, not proprietary tech.
Best next market: Already huge on downloads in India but badly under-monetized there — the opportunity is a better-priced, better-paid regional competitor, not an unclaimed market.

5. Replika

Companion — Cautionary Case

Long-term AI "friend/partner" persona for emotional support and companionship.

Traction
Down from ~$14M ARR (2024) to ~$4.8-5M (2026) as newer entrants took share. ~40M cumulative users. €5M GDPR fine in Europe.
Business model
Subscription (Replika Pro) plus in-app purchases.
Why it's here
Category pioneer (2017) whose lead eroded once competitors matched model quality and added voice. Proof that first-mover status alone doesn't hold in this space.
Lesson for BGV: A well-executed localized fast-follower with better model quality can overtake an incumbent. Also a live warning on data-protection exposure (GDPR precedent relevant to Brazil's LGPD and similar regimes).

6. AI Companion Apps — Zeta, Tipsy Chat, ChatBox, Crushie AI, Emochi

Companion — Highest Yield, Highest Risk

Romantic/companion AI chat, often with voice and avatar/video features.

Traction
H1 2026: Zeta ~$33M, Tipsy Chat ~$15.2M, ChatBox ~$13M, Crushie ~$8.8M, Emochi ~$7.5M. Category ~$5.01B in 2026 (18.1% CAGR). Top 10% of apps capture 89% of revenue.
Business model
Subscription plus heavy microtransactions (message credits, voice minutes, image unlocks).
The proof-of-concept for this exact thesis
Zeta (Korean, Scatter Lab) fast-followed the US category and won by localizing hard into Japan — now Japan is its #1 download market, usage up 150%+ YoY.
Barrier to replicate
Low tech barrier (LLM + voice + avatar is off-the-shelf), moderate CAC barrier, but significant App Store policy and reputational risk.
Best next market: MENA and India — real latent demand (loneliness, dating stigma, arranged-marriage culture) that Western and Korean players haven't localized for, but the least brand-safe option on this list.

7. Suno

Creative / Generative — Hard to Clone

Text-to-song AI music generator — full songs with vocals from a prompt.

Traction
2M+ subscribers, ARR reported $130-300M. $400M Series D May 2026 at $5.4B valuation (up from $2.45B Nov 2025).
Business model
Freemium/subscription tiers for generation credits and commercial rights.
Barrier to replicate
High — competitive music generation needs large licensed/scraped audio datasets (Suno itself is in active litigation over this) and serious ML talent.
Best next market: Latin America (reggaeton, Brazilian funk), Sub-Saharan Africa (Afrobeats/Amapiano) — Suno's model quality lags on non-English lyrics and regional genres. Real gap, but only realistic for a well-funded, legally careful team.

8. Gamma

Productivity

Turns a prompt or outline into a polished slide deck, document, or webpage instantly.

Traction
$100M+ ARR, 70M users, 600K+ paying subscribers, $2.1B valuation after a16z-led $68M Series B (Nov 2025).
Business model
Freemium subscription.
Unlock
Output looks professionally designed by default — solves the tedium ChatGPT text dumps don't.
Barrier to replicate
Moderate — the pattern is replicable, but 3+ years of design-quality refinement and user trust is a real head start.
Best next market: India and Southeast Asia — large English-medium student and early-career population, weak local competition in this specific niche, low localization complexity.

9. Duolingo (Max)

Education — Model to Imitate, Not Clone

Language learning app; Max tier adds an AI conversational tutor and AI-generated exercises.

Traction
Q1 2026 revenue $292M (+27% YoY), full-year guide ~$1.2B (+16%). Chess hit ~7M DAU within a year of launch.
Business model
Freemium/subscription (Super, Max) with ad-supported free tier.
Barrier to replicate
High for a direct clone — 15-year content and gamification-data moat. Much lower for a narrower AI-tutor vertical it doesn't own.
Best next market: Not a fast-follow target itself. The mechanic (gamification + AI tutor) applied to vocational/professional skills training in India, the Philippines or Sub-Saharan Africa — where human tutoring is scarce — is the real opportunity.

10. Perplexity

Search — Hard to Clone

AI-native search — cited, conversational answers instead of a list of links.

Traction
~100M MAU (2026). ARR ~$450M (Mar 2026) trending toward ~$656M full-year. Moved to subscription-first, revenue jumped ~50% in weeks.
Business model
Freemium subscription (Pro), ads removed Feb 2026.
Barrier to replicate
Very high — competing with Google's index and frontier model quality is the hardest clone on this list without serious capital.
Best next market: India (Hindi, Tamil, Bengali) or Indonesia/Vietnam — genuine gap in local-language quality, but only viable as a narrow vertical/local-language wedge, not a general clone.

11. Rosebud

Wellness — Early Stage

AI-guided reflective journaling — asks personalized follow-up questions, tracks mood/patterns over time.

Traction
$12.6M total funding, 7,500+ paying customers disclosed, 500M+ words journaled. No public revenue figure — sub-$10M scale currently.
Business model
Subscription, $12.99/mo.
Barrier to replicate
Low tech barrier — but the category itself is unproven at scale. Flagged honestly as promising idea, not proven playbook.
Best next market: Japan/South Korea — deep cultural roots in reflective journaling (Japanese "nikki" tradition) with few AI-native tools localized for the tone and formality norms those languages need.

12. Otter.ai / Granola

Prosumer — Boundary Case

Auto-join calls, transcribe, summarize, generate action items from meetings.

Traction
Otter: Pro $16.99/user/mo, Business $30/user/mo. Granola: $14/user/mo, well-funded, popular with consultants/executives.
Business model
Per-seat subscription — closer to prosumer/SMB than pure consumer.
Unlock
Granola's "no visible bot" (records passively vs. joining as a visible participant) solved a real social-awkwardness problem Otter didn't.
Barrier to replicate
Moderate — transcription tech is commodity, differentiation is UX and calendar/CRM integration depth.
Best next market: India or the Philippines — huge outsourced/remote workforce, English-medium but accented, where US-trained transcription models are measurably weaker.

13. Iris Dating

Dating — High Cold-Start Risk

AI-native matchmaking that learns preference/attraction patterns from swipe behavior before matching.

Market context
Global dating-app revenue ~$3.24B (2026), US ~$1.45B. Incumbents Tinder ($58.3M), Bumble ($28.96M), Hinge ($18.54M) still dominate; Iris and similar AI-native players are the emerging wedge.
Business model
Freemium/subscription, standard dating-app playbook.
Barrier to replicate
High — dating is a classic network-effects business; even a superior product faces a cold-start problem requiring city-by-city launch marketing.
Best next market: India (existing apps like TrulyMadly/Aisle are weak on AI) or Gulf/MENA (demand for privacy-respecting, culturally appropriate matchmaking). Hardest category here to actually launch, despite a real gap.

14. PhotoRoom

E-commerce Tooling

AI background removal and product photo generation for e-commerce sellers.

Traction
$150M+ ARR (Jan 2026, +100% YoY), 200M+ downloads, $75.3M total funding, $500M+ valuation.
Business model
Freemium subscription, tiered for individual sellers vs. small businesses.
Unlock
Purpose-built for e-commerce sellers rather than a generic photo editor — a specific, high-value, recurring workflow.
Barrier to replicate
Low-moderate — the AI is well-understood; the real work is non-technical seller UX and marketplace-specific format integrations.
Best next market: Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines) and Latin America (Brazil, Mexico) — booming Shopee/Tokopedia/Mercado Livre seller populations underserved by anything this sophisticated.
All figures are third-party estimates (Sensor Tower, Business of Apps, PitchBook, Sacra, getlatka, company press coverage) as of August 2026, not audited financials — treat as directional. Compiled for Olly / BGV strategy review.
