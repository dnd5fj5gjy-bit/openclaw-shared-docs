Modern Savage Organic / IG Stories

Then vs now,
told in stories

Nine organic Story posts built off the "how food used to be earned vs the modern world" angle from the Reel brief - same rule set, adapted for the Stories surface: quick, native, interactive, not a shrink-fit of the Reel.

SurfaceIG Stories
Format9:16, native
CadenceDaily, 1 story
BrandCaption only
The rule carried over from the Reel

Both eras stay equally beautiful

No story should read as "good footage vs bad footage." Warm/primal and cold/modern are graded differently, never shot at different quality. Same as the Reel: Modern Savage is never shown on screen - it only appears in the caption, as the way back.

01 / The nine posts

Ready-to-post concepts

01

The Hook, Standalone

Text card
VisualA single still from the "Then" half - hands turning meat over an open fire, embers rising. No motion needed.
Text"How our grandparents got their food."
StickerNone. Let the line and the image sit. This is the teaser before the Reel drops.
CaptionModern Savage is the way back.
02

The Flip, Standalone

Text card
VisualA single still from the "Now" half - plastic-wrapped meat on a styrofoam tray under cold light.
Text"...vs the modern world."
StickerNone. Post the day after story 01 as the answer to the question it left open.
CaptionSomewhere along the way, we lost this.
03

This or That

Poll sticker
VisualSplit frame - fire and open land on the left, fluorescent aisle on the right.
Text"Which one are you built for?"
StickerPoll sticker, options labelled by the two images rather than words. Outcome is obvious - that is the point, it makes people tap.
CaptionYou already know the answer.
04

Guess The Era

Quiz sticker
VisualA tightly cropped, ambiguous macro shot - could read as either era at first glance (e.g. hands and fire, close enough to hide context).
Text"Then or now?"
StickerQuiz sticker, two options: Then / Now. Next story reveals the wide shot and the correct answer.
CaptionNo caption needed - let the quiz carry it.
05

Countdown to the Reel

Countdown sticker
VisualBest single frame from Part 1 of the Reel - the drone shot over the green valley at golden hour.
Text"The way back drops Friday."
StickerCountdown sticker to the Reel's publish time. Let followers set a reminder.
CaptionModern Savage is the way back.
06

Reel Repost, Day Of

Native share
VisualThe Reel itself, shared natively to Stories the moment it goes live.
TextKeep the Reel's own on-screen text - no extra overlay needed.
StickerLink sticker to the Reel if driving traffic off-post; otherwise none, let it play.
CaptionIt is live.
07

Behind the Grade

BTS clip
VisualSplit-screen colour-grading comparison - same shot, warm grade next to cold grade, shown side by side.
Text"Same shot. Same quality. Opposite temperature."
StickerNone, or an emoji slider ("which feels warmer") if you want a light interaction.
CaptionThat is the whole trick - the contrast is never quality, only colour.
08

Hook Line Carousel

Multi-frame
VisualThree consecutive story frames, same still image (community eating around the fire) held throughout.
TextFrame 1: "Three generations ago, this was normal." Frame 2: "Now we just unwrap it." Frame 3: "Your body still remembers this."
StickerNone. Tests three hook lines from the Reel brief's option bank in sequence, cheaply, before committing one to paid.
CaptionNot used - the lines are the whole post.
09

Ask Us Anything: Then vs Now

Question sticker
VisualWarm-graded still, hunter or riders on open plains.
Text"What did food look like where you grew up?"
StickerQuestion sticker. Reshare the best replies as follow-up stories over the next day or two - free, on-narrative UGC.
CaptionNone needed - the question is the post.
No product on screen No voiceover Brand in caption only Same warm/cold grade rule as the Reel
02 / In one breath

Stories carry the same "then vs now" narrative as the Reel but do the jobs Stories are actually good at - teasing, polling, countdown, BTS, UGC prompts - instead of squeezing the Reel down to fit. Post 01-02 as a teaser pair before the Reel, 03-04 for engagement any day, 05-06 around the Reel's launch, 07-09 as evergreen filler across the week.
