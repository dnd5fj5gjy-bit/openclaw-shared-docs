01 · Applies to all five

Rules for every graphic

Read once. Every brief below assumes these.

One neutral master, then a batch per partner

Build each graphic once as a neutral master with no Ted's Health branding. It is then produced per partner by changing only the six fields below. Everything else is locked. Partners receive finished files, not editable templates.

#FieldSpec
1Partner logoVector (SVG or EPS), or PNG at 2000px or larger on transparent background
2Partner coloursPrimary and secondary hex. Check text contrast meets WCAG AA and adjust tints if it does not
3Partner nameTrading name as it appears on their platform
4LocationTown, and address or hours where a print piece needs it
5Link and QRPartner's tracked link, shown as short text under the button. Static QR encoding the same tracked link, bottom right. Per partner, per service, per graphic
6Price line (optional, off by default)One figure for the service, taken from the partner's live checkout price on the day. Never a recommended price, never per dose, never a wholesale figure. Format: "from £[x]"

Layout anatomy (feed post)

Partner logo, top left
Headline
Image or illustration + one-line support copy
CTA button + link text · QR
Small print + provider line
  • Typography: one clean sans-serif family, headline bold, body regular. Partner's own font if they supply one, otherwise Montserrat.
  • Headline: largest element, maximum two lines, sentence case.
  • CTA button: a filled shape in the partner's primary colour with the button text inside. It is a visual label only. The link lives in the caption and in the text under the button.
  • Space: generous. If in doubt, remove an element rather than add one.
  • Small print: minimum 22px on the feed size, readable at phone size. Never hidden or low contrast.
  • Colour: the partner's palette leads. The Ted's Health navy, teal and gold are not used on partner graphics.

Do and don't

Do

  • Promote the test, the consultation and the process
  • Use real, everyday adults in lifestyle settings, or simple approved illustration
  • Use only the wording written in the brief
  • Keep tone plain, calm and professional
  • Use British spelling and hyphens, no em dashes
  • Write descriptive alt text with no medicine names

Never

  • Name or show any prescription medicine or brand, in copy, hashtags, alt text or file names
  • Show pens, needles, syringes, vials, tablets or packaging
  • Use before/after images, tape measures, scales, shirtless or physique shots
  • Promise or imply results, timeframes, percentages or statistics
  • Use "fast", "instant", "guaranteed", "cure" or urgency language
  • Show discount codes, free offers or "first month free"
  • Use patient testimonials or influencer quotes
  • Show anyone who looks under 18

Badges and provider line

  • No CQC, LegitScript or "Backed by Bear Grylls Ventures" badges on partner graphics. Those belong to Ted's Health as the provider, not the partner, and the partner brand leads on anything the patient sees.
  • Trust line allowed: "Reviewed by a UK-registered clinician" and "Private and confidential".
  • Provider line in the small print, one line, no logo. Proposed wording: "Clinical assessment, prescribing and blood test review are provided by Ted's Health (GSET Health Ltd), a CQC-registered provider." Helen is confirming whether this is required on marketing assets. Leave the line in place on the master so it can be switched on or off in one step.

File naming

Use neutral service words only: [partner]-blood-test-feed-v1.png, [partner]-hormone-check-story-v1.png, [partner]-ed-consult-linkedin-v1.png, [partner]-weight-mgmt-feed-v1.png, [partner]-shop-webhero-v1.png. No medicine names, ever.

Know your numbers

The lead product. Available to every clinic today, so this goes to every partner.

What it promotes

The blood test range, with a clinician reviewing the results. It does not claim what a panel detects or diagnoses.

Small print
Adults 18+. [Provider line]
Visual direction
  • Lead image: a boxed test kit on a kitchen table or worktop, or a phone showing a clean results screen (no real data, no invented numbers). Warm natural light.

03 · Brief 2
Men's hormone health · Start now

Tired, low, not yourself?

The testosterone replacement service, advertised as a hormone check and a clinical review, never as the treatment.

Do not use "TRT" or "testosterone therapy" on the graphic. Testosterone is a controlled prescription-only medicine, so promoting the treatment itself is not allowed. The graphic promotes the blood test and the review. Whether "TRT" is acceptable as a label at all is still with the professional advisers, so the working title is Men's hormone health check.
What it promotes

A testosterone blood test followed by a review with a UK-registered clinician. A plan is discussed only if appropriate. Men 18+ with general symptoms. Not a bodybuilding or performance audience.

Copy on the graphic (draft)
Headline
Tired, low, not yourself?
Support
It could be worth checking your hormones. Start with a blood test and a review by a UK-registered clinician, who will talk you through your results and options.
Button
Check your levels
Small print
Men 18+. [Provider line]
Visual direction
  • A real man, 35 to 55, in an ordinary moment: early morning at a kitchen counter, sitting on the edge of a sofa, or a quiet moment in a car before work. Tired but not distressed. Warm, slightly muted light.
  • Or no person at all: the boxed test kit and a phone on a bedside table or kitchen table.
  • Tone is empathetic and unshaming. The image should say "a normal person having an off spell", not "a problem to fix".
Avoid specifically
  • Gym, muscle, shirtless or physique imagery of any kind
  • Implying that these symptoms mean low testosterone. The copy says "could be worth checking", nothing stronger
  • Words like "boost", "low T", "optimise", "gains", "performance", "man enough"
  • Gel, injection, ampoule or pack imagery

Advertises a private online consultation. Discretion is the message.

Public graphic only for open-access partners. Some partners have ED set to doctor-initiated. For those, no public graphic is made, and they get in-clinic referral material only. Check the partner's ED access setting on the intake form before building.
What it promotes

A private, confidential online consultation, reviewed by a UK-registered clinician. Men 18+.

Copy on the graphic (draft)

Support
A private online consultation, reviewed by a UK-registered clinician. Discreet from start to finish.
Button
Start your private consultation
Small print
Men 18+. [Provider line]
Visual direction
  • Restrained and abstract. A phone in a hand at a quiet desk, an unmarked front door, a man at ease at a kitchen table with a mug. Soft, cool-warm light.

05 · Brief 4
Weight management · Design only, gated

A clinician-led approach to managing your weight

Highest-risk category in the pack. Built now, released later.

Not released to any partner until all three are true: their contract is signed, weight loss is switched on for their clinic on the platform, and Dr Andrews has approved the copy and the visuals with Helen having reviewed it. Until then this brief is design work only, and the files stay in an internal folder.
What it promotes

A clinician-led weight management consultation with an eligibility check. It promotes the process, not a medicine. "You pay nothing until your plan is approved" is true for this pathway and is the strongest compliant message available. Adults 18+ who could meet the eligibility criteria. This brief has one version at this stage. Men's and women's versions can follow once the imagery is cleared.

Copy on the graphic (draft)
Headline
A clinician-led approach to managing your weight.
Support
Check whether you are eligible in a few minutes. A UK-registered clinician reviews every application, and you pay nothing until your plan is approved.
Button
Check your eligibility
Small print
Adults 18+. Eligibility criteria apply. [Provider line]. Exact eligibility wording to come from Dr Andrews.
Visual direction
  • Keep bodies out of it. A phone showing a simple eligibility screen, a kitchen table with a mug and a notepad, walking shoes by a door, a laptop on a desk in warm daylight.
  • Where a person is shown, use a real, everyday adult in a normal setting, shot from the face up or from behind, with no focus on body shape.
  • A clean approved illustration is an acceptable alternative.
Avoid specifically
  • Any medicine name, "jab", "injection", "pen", "needle", or any product or packaging image
  • Weight lost, pounds, stones, percentages or any timeframe
  • Before/after images, tape measures, scales, mirrors, body-focused shots
  • People who are visibly not overweight in weight-loss material
  • Body-image pressure language such as "summer body", "get in shape", "transform"
  • Mounjaro, Wegovy, tirzepatide, semaglutide, GLP-1 and "fat jab", in any form, including hashtags and alt text
06 · Brief 5
The whole shop · Start now

Private health services, online

One graphic that introduces the whole shop and sends people to it. Used at launch and any time the partner wants to point at everything.

What it promotes

The partner's online health shop as a whole: a place to book tests and consultations privately. It shows only the services that partner has switched on.

Make two versions of every format. Version A shows blood tests, men's hormone health and ED. Version B adds weight management, and is only used for partners cleared under Brief 4. If a partner has ED set to doctor-initiated, Version A drops the ED tile too. Match the version to the partner's intake form.
Copy on the graphic (draft)
Headline
Private health services, online.
Support
Blood tests, men's hormone health and ED consultations, all reviewed by a UK-registered clinician. [Weight management, Version B only.]
Service row
Three or four tiles with a simple icon and a short label: Blood tests, Men's hormone health, ED consultation, Weight management (Version B only). Labels only, no descriptions.
Button
Visit our health shop
Optional
"Not sure where to start? Take the 2-minute health check." Only where the partner has the public Health Quiz switched on. Otherwise leave it out.
Small print
Adults 18+. [Provider line]
Visual direction
  • Layout-led rather than photo-led. A clean partner-colour background, the partner logo prominent, the service tiles in a tidy row or 2 x 2 grid.
  • Icons: one consistent line-icon set. Use a droplet or test tube for blood tests, a simple person outline for hormone health, a phone or speech bubble for ED consultation, a heart or leaf for weight management. No pills, needles, pens or body shapes.
  • One soft lifestyle photo is allowed as a background or side panel (a bright kitchen, a home desk). It must not compete with the tiles.
  • Web hero (1920 x 800): headline left, tiles right, generous empty space. Mobile (1080 x 1080): headline top, tiles below.
Avoid specifically
  • Any individual service claim, price or offer on this graphic. It is the shop front, not a sales page
  • Listing weight management on a partner who has not been cleared for it
  • Any Ted's Health logo or "Powered by" lockup. That decision has not been made
