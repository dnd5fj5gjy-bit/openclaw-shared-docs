## Recommended categories — green
1. **72-hour emergency kits for home, car and family.** Seventy2 / Basecamp / Roadster. This is the centre of Bear's expertise and 25 years of teaching. Their "everyday heroes" framing maps directly onto his Scouting and Be Military Fit audiences.
2. **First aid and trauma kits.** Cleanest first-responder tie-in, no licence conflict, low price point for mass distribution.
3. **Vehicle and overland preparedness.** Roadster, Overlander, Extractor tow strap, DOT fleet kit. Direct content dovetail with **Adventure Garage** (S2 production starts Sept, car builds Oct/Nov, adventure segments Feb-Apr '27; S1 already carried Goodyear and RealTruck integrations).
4. **Water purification and treatment within kits.** Authentic, but see amber note on Water2 before any standalone filtration SKU.
5. **Signalling, comms and light.** NOAA radio, beacons, torches. Clean.
6. **Shelter, warmth and fire.** Bivvys, blankets, fire-starting. Clean.

## Amber — check before committing
- **Knives, multitools, backpacks, duffels.** **Black Swan Manufacturing Inc.** holds the BG gear licence covering camping gear, knives, tents, sleeping bags, backpacks and multitools (Amazon BG Store). Dormant in practice (no new BG goods in ~21 months as of Dec 2025, near-zero recent royalty), but live on paper. These are exactly Uncharted's core SKUs, so scope needs a read before a collab line touches them. Contact: John Law.
- **Portable power, jump starters, solar.** The EcoFlow / BRANDVIEW negotiation is live with Hal Burg. Keep power out of scope for now.
- **Watches.** Uncharted resells Garmin. Luminox holds Bear's watch deal. Out.
- **Water filtration as a standalone product.** Bear is informal ambassador to Water2 with 5-10% equity talks open. In-kit purification tabs/straws are unlikely to trouble it, but a branded filter product would.

## Deal read
No MG on the table is not unusual at their size (~$8M raised). Equity plus royalty is the more interesting structure here given they're mid-conversation with a strategic. If that deal completes, equity is where the upside sits. Worth understanding who the strategic is and what stage that's at before pricing anything.
