Psalm 144

Power that comes back in the field.

Swappable cells for drones, controllers and launch systems. No charging, no downtime, no waiting on a generator.

“Blessed be the Lord my strength, who trains my hands for war, and my fingers for battle.”

Psalm 144:1

Swapped, not charged

A spent cell comes out and a fresh one goes in. Seconds, not hours, and nothing has to be plugged into anything.

Stores without degrading

Stacked on a shelf or sealed in a trunk box, stock stays ready. Depot to deployment without a charge cycle in between.

One cell, many systems

The same unit powers the aircraft, the ground controller and the launch electronics.
