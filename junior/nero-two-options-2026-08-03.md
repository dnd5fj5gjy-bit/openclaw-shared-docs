# Bear Grylls x Nero - two routes

**Outline terms, subject to contract. 3 August 2026.**

Two alternative structures. They are not cumulative, and either can stand on its own.

**Route 1** is the full partnership: Bear works across the whole Nero brand and is paid out of the growth.

**Route 2** is a single co-branded capsule with Modern Savage, promoted by Bear and sold through the Modern Savage store. Smaller, faster, and a way to see what the two audiences do together before either side commits to anything longer.

---

# Route 1. Partnership

## 1.1 Equity on signing

**5% of Nero**, ordinary shares, granted on signature to a Bear Grylls vehicle.

No fee and no retainer.

## 1.2 Baseline

**Baseline = Nero's Net Revenue for the twelve months ending on the last full calendar month before the term sheet is signed.**

Set once, evidenced by the Shopify export and the company's accounts, and fixed for the life of the deal.

**Net Revenue** is defined once and used identically wherever it appears: gross sales, less returns, refunds, discounts, chargebacks, shipping charged to customers, and sales taxes.

## 1.3 Growth share

**25% of Net Revenue above the Baseline**, calculated and reported quarterly.

Growth only. Revenue up to the Baseline is untouched.

## 1.4 Settlement, cash or shares

Within **30 days of each quarterly statement**, Bear elects whether that quarter's amount is settled in cash, in Nero shares, or in any combination of the two. The election is Bear's, quarter by quarter, and may differ each quarter.

Shares are allotted within 20 business days of the election. Where they are not allotted, the amount remains payable in cash.

## 1.5 Share price on a settlement in shares

**Price = the lower of:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **A** | **$12,000,000 pre-money**, fixed for the term |
| **B** | **2.0x Nero's trailing twelve-month Net Revenue** at the date of that quarter's statement |

The quarter's amount is divided by that price to give the shareholding issued.

**Illustrative arithmetic only, figures not proposed:** on a Baseline of $3.0M and year one Net Revenue of $4.0M, growth is $1.0M and the growth share is $250,000. Trailing revenue of $4.0M gives $8.0M under B, which is lower than A, so $250,000 divided by $8.0M is 3.1% of the company.

## 1.6 Ceiling and term

The growth share runs until the **earlier** of:

- Bear's aggregate holding reaching **15% of the company**, or
- **three years** from signature.

At that point the growth share ends and nothing continues automatically. Bear has an option, exercisable at the time, to extend by up to two years. The name licence runs for the same period as the growth share and ends with it.

**The maximum aggregate holding under this structure is 15%.**

## 1.7 What Bear provides, each year

- Two shoot days
- Six posts across Bear's own channels
- Whitelisting of approved assets for Nero's paid media
- Three introductions to Bear's retail and distribution contacts
- A launch announcement and a supporting quote

Podcast and press mentions are best efforts and are not contractual.

## 1.8 Reporting

Quarterly statement within 30 days of each quarter end, with the underlying Shopify and accounting export, and an annual reconciliation to the filed accounts.

## 1.9 Remedies, both ways

**Nero:** the name licence suspends, with 30 days to cure, on a late quarterly statement, a failure to allot elected shares, a failure to pay any amount elected in cash, or a late annual reconciliation.

**Bear:** a material failure to deliver the annual commitments halves the growth share rate for the following four quarters. A second consecutive year of material failure allows Nero to terminate the growth share and the licence. A clean year resets the position.

Neither remedy affects the 5% or any shares already issued.

## 1.10 Standard protections

Pre-emption on new issues and a minimum holding floor. Tag-along on a sale. Where shares are issued to a third party below the price in 1.5, that lower price applies to Bear's outstanding elections.

On a change of control or an institutional funding round, Bear elects either to settle all outstanding amounts up to the 15% ceiling at the price in 1.5, or to continue unaffected.

Quarterly management accounts, cap table on request, and a board observer seat at 10%.

The contracting company holds the trademark, the store and the customer data, and these are not transferred outside it during the term.

# Route 2. Nero x Modern Savage collaboration

Modern Savage is Bear's own brand. A co-branded Nero x Modern Savage capsule, promoted by Bear and sold through the Modern Savage store, puts Nero product in front of Bear's audience directly and tells both sides what that audience actually does.

## 2.1 The product

A short co-branded capsule drawn from Nero's existing range: shorts and boxers. Nero manufactures.

Artwork and product approval by both sides before production.

## 2.2 How it is sold

**Dropship.** Nero funds production, holds the stock, and picks, packs and ships each order. Modern Savage sells through its own store and carries the marketing. No minimum order quantity.

## 2.3 Revenue share

**Modern Savage retains 40% of Net Receipts** on sales through its own channels. Nero invoices the balance against orders shipped.

**Net Receipts** means the amount actually received, being the price after any discount actually given, less sales taxes, refunds and chargebacks. Delivery charges paid by the customer sit outside the split and pass to Nero in full.

Invoicing monthly against orders shipped with a line-level report. Refunds net off the following period.

## 2.4 If Nero sells the capsule on its own channels

**10% of Net Sales to Modern Savage**, on the same definition, reported and paid quarterly.

## 2.5 What Bear provides

- A launch announcement and a supporting quote
- Three posts across Bear's own channels
- Whitelisting of the approved launch assets for Nero's paid media during the campaign window

## 2.6 Use of Bear's name and image

The agreed co-branded pack and campaign credit is the extent of the permission granted. **Every other use, including campaigns, listings, social, email and affiliate, needs written approval in advance, case by case.** No general or implied permission is given.

## 2.7 Term and territory

One capsule, twelve months from first sale, then by agreement. Territory to be agreed, with a view to starting in a single market.

## 2.8 Practical points

Product liability and recall cover in place before first sale, naming both parties. Each side is responsible for its own channel's customer service and refunds. Stock, artwork and listings on exit to be agreed in the paperwork.

---

**Both routes are open. They are alternatives rather than stages, and either can start on its own.**
