# Bear Grylls x Nero - two routes

**Outline terms, subject to contract. 3 August 2026.**

---

# Route 1. Partnership

**Equity.** 5% of Nero, granted on signing. No fee and no retainer.

**Growth share.** 25% of the growth in Nero's revenue above where it sits in the twelve months before we start.

**Cash or shares.** Each quarter Bear chooses whether to take that quarter's amount in cash, in Nero shares, or a mix of the two.

**The share price.** The lower of a $12m valuation, fixed for the term, and 2.0x Nero's trailing twelve-month revenue at the time.

**The ceiling.** It runs until Bear reaches 15% of the company, or for three years, whichever comes first.

**Bear.** Two shoot days a year, six posts across his own channels, whitelisting of approved assets for Nero's paid media, three introductions to Bear's retail and distribution contacts, and a launch announcement with a supporting quote.

---

# Route 2. Nero x Modern Savage collaboration

**The product.** Shorts and boxers, co-branded, from Nero's existing product. More by agreement.

**How it works.** Nero makes it, holds the stock and ships the orders. Modern Savage sells it.

**The split.** Modern Savage takes 35% of sales of the co-branded range, wherever it sells, on either store.

**Bear.** A shoot for the campaign, a launch announcement, posts across his own channels, and his accounts made available for Nero to run the campaign assets as paid advertising.

**Media.** Nero plans and pays for the advertising.

**Bear's name and image** beyond the agreed pack and campaign credit is by approval each time.
