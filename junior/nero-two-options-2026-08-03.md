# Bear Grylls x Nero - two routes

**Outline terms, subject to contract. 3 August 2026.**

---

# Route 1. Partnership

## 1.1 Equity on signing

**5% of Nero**, ordinary shares, granted on signature to a Bear Grylls vehicle.

No fee and no retainer.

## 1.2 Baseline

**Baseline = Nero's Net Revenue for the twelve months ending on the last full calendar month before the term sheet is signed.** Evidenced by the Shopify export and the company's accounts, and fixed for the life of the deal.

**Net Revenue** = gross sales, less returns, refunds, discounts, chargebacks, shipping charged to customers, and sales taxes. The same definition applies wherever it appears.

## 1.3 Growth share

**25% of Net Revenue above the Baseline**, calculated and reported quarterly.

## 1.4 Settlement, cash or shares

Within **30 days of each quarterly statement**, Bear elects whether that quarter's amount is settled in cash, in Nero shares, or in any combination of the two. The election is Bear's and may differ each quarter.

Shares are allotted within 20 business days of the election. Where they are not allotted, the amount remains payable in cash.

## 1.5 Share price on a settlement in shares

**Price = the lower of:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **A** | **$12,000,000 pre-money**, fixed for the term |
| **B** | **2.0x Nero's trailing twelve-month Net Revenue** at the date of that quarter's statement |

The quarter's amount is divided by that price to give the shareholding issued.

## 1.6 Ceiling and term

The growth share runs until the **earlier** of:

- Bear's aggregate holding reaching **15% of the company**, or
- **three years** from signature.

Bear then has an option to extend by up to two years. The name licence runs for the same period as the growth share and ends with it.

## 1.7 What Bear provides, each year

- Two shoot days
- Six posts across Bear's own channels
- Whitelisting of approved assets for Nero's paid media
- Three introductions to Bear's retail and distribution contacts
- A launch announcement and a supporting quote

Podcast and press mentions are best efforts and are not contractual.

## 1.8 Reporting

Quarterly statement within 30 days of each quarter end, with the underlying Shopify and accounting export, and an annual reconciliation to the filed accounts.

## 1.9 Remedies

**Nero:** the name licence suspends, with 30 days to cure, on a late quarterly statement, a failure to allot elected shares, a failure to pay any amount elected in cash, or a late annual reconciliation.

**Bear:** a material failure to deliver the annual commitments halves the growth share rate for the following four quarters. A second consecutive year of material failure allows Nero to terminate the growth share and the licence. A clean year resets the position.

Neither remedy affects the 5% or any shares already issued.

## 1.10 Standard protections

Pre-emption on new issues and a minimum holding floor. Tag-along on a sale. Where shares are issued to a third party below the price in 1.5, that lower price applies to Bear's outstanding elections.

On a change of control or an institutional funding round, Bear elects either to settle all outstanding amounts up to the 15% ceiling at the price in 1.5, or to continue unaffected.

Quarterly management accounts, cap table on request, and a board observer seat at 10%.

The contracting company holds the trademark, the store and the customer data, and these are not transferred outside it during the term.

# Route 2. Nero x Modern Savage collaboration

**The product.** Shorts and boxers, co-branded, from Nero's existing product. More by agreement.

**How it works.** Nero makes it, holds the stock and ships the orders. Modern Savage sells it.

**The split.** Modern Savage takes **40% of sales of the co-branded range**, wherever it sells, on either store.

**Bear.** A shoot for the campaign, a launch announcement, posts across his own channels, and his accounts made available for Nero to run the campaign assets as paid advertising.

**Media.** Nero plans and pays for the advertising.

**Bear's name and image** beyond the agreed pack and campaign credit is by approval each time.
