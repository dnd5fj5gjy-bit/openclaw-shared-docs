# BMF: a first plan

**Draft for Jesse's approval. Nothing has been sent to Farren or to anyone at GoRuck.**

---

Farren,

Good to speak last week. I said I would put something down on paper so we have a starting point to
argue with, so here it is. Treat it as a first draft rather than a proposal. If you think the order is
wrong, say so, because you know this market better than I do.

**Where BMF actually is.** We have deliberately cut back to two sites, Hyde Park and Battersea, while we
work out the right direction. What has survived is the part that matters: the name, the association with
my father, relationships across the parks we used to operate in, and an email list built up over a very
long time. The brand is in better condition than the business is. That is the asset and it is the reason
I want to be careful about who we hand it to.

**What we are looking for.** Someone motivated who will be a good steward of it, who we can support and
get behind rather than manage. We are not looking to earn out of this in the early stages. We want a
community first and an audience first, and we will work out the commercial side once there is something
real to be commercial about.

**Phase one, the pilot.** Your idea, and I think it is the right place to start. A ninety day pilot at
one site, most likely Battersea, adding rucks and weighted vests into the upper tiers of the existing
bib system. It tests whether tabbing and rucking land with a British park audience before we build
anything around it, and it gives us three months of content and photography for whatever comes next.

**Phase two, the relaunch event.** A single large event to bring the community back, run properly and
filmed properly. Your instinct that we do not need to make money on the first one is right. What we need
is the turnout, the footage and the proof. If the format is a military games, with tiers, and with the
army, police and fire services invited to compete against each other, that is a far stronger idea than
another race, and it is one nobody in Britain is currently running.

**Phase three, what it becomes.** The version I keep coming back to is BMF as the thing that gets people
ready. HYROX owns the race. We would own the standard: if you are training to pass selection, to join
the police, the fire service or the army, you train the BMF way, and gyms across the country can carry
that as an affiliation. Your visiting gym idea is how we would build it, exactly as HYROX did.

**On GoRuck.** Your connection there is a real advantage and I would like to use it properly. Kit at no
cost is a genuine saving and we should take it. But if we build an event together I think it should be
structured rather than informal, so that both sides know what they are getting. What I would want to
protect is that BMF owns the event brand and the entrants, and GoRuck come in as a named sponsor on a
defined term. That is not me being difficult about it. It is because I think this could be big enough
that it will matter in three years who owned what, and it is much easier to agree now than later.

**How we would work together.** That is the conversation I would rather have face to face than in an
email. What I will say is that I am not looking for someone to run a job for us. I am looking for
someone to build something with real ownership of it, and I would want you properly incentivised in
whatever we agree.

**Next step.** Let us get a date in London in the next fortnight. Come back on this with anything you
would change, add or throw out, and bring what you think the pilot would actually need to run.

Jesse
