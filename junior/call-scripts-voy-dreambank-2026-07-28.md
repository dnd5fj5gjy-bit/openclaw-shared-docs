1. **Voy at 15:00 is billed as a creative review. Treat it as a shape review.** If you give notes on the creative, you have quietly agreed Bear is the face of their campaign. Bear's actual goal is to be a partner with equity, not talent for hire.
2. **Do not give a verdict on the creative today.** Say it's strong, ask for the deck, promise written notes this week. You lose nothing and you buy the room.
3. **Say nothing about Ted's numbers and nothing about selling Ted's.** That is a separate conversation, with CJ and Raemy, later. Ted's is the leverage.
4. **DreamBank at 16:00 is the opposite problem.** Bear already loves it. The job is not to be sold, it is to find out what is actually being asked for: his name, his time, his audience, or his money.
5. **One line to say on each call.** Voy: *"Before we get into the creative, help me understand the shape - is Bear the face of a Voy campaign, or a partner whose own brand sits alongside yours?"* DreamBank: *"What's the ask, specifically - is this Bear's name, his time, or his capital?"*

**Why this works:** on both calls the other side has prepared a presentation and you have prepared a question. The question is worth more. You are not there to react well, you are there to find out what is really being proposed before anyone commits Bear to anything.

# CALL 1 — VOY, 15:00-15:45 BST

**Google Meet:** meet.google.com/mrt-vvox-hri

**In the room:** Maximilian Wenger (Director of Men's Health, Voy) · Elisabeth Stone (leads content at Voy, she is presenting the concepts) · CJ · you. Raemy is on holiday. Bear is not on the call.

**Your seat:** CJ put you on this specifically as the person who leads creative for BGV. So creative input is expected of you. That is exactly why the trap below is a real one.

### What you need to know going in

- Voy: UK's leading men's health platform. **£675M annualised revenue, 120% year-on-year growth, largest private TRT clinic in the UK.** Their words, from Max's first email.
- They asked for a **"flagship partner who can bring credibility and reach"** to TRT. That is a sponsorship request in the way it is worded.
- **They already run Dr Rangan Chatterjee** on their preventative health brand. That is the tell. There is an existing template for "celebrity health partner" at Voy, and the concepts you are about to see are very likely that template with Bear dropped into it.
- The NDA is signed both ways (Raemy confirmed 6 July). You can talk freely about the partnership. That does not mean you should talk about Ted's.
- Raemy has already planted the leverage: she told Max on 6 July that **other parties are interested in collaborating with Bear / Ted's Health.** You do not need to repeat it hard. It is already in the water.
- **You have not seen the concepts.** Max said Elisabeth will walk you through them live. So you will be reacting in real time to work you have never read. Assume that is deliberate, and do not let it produce a yes.

### The trap, in one sentence

Bear's goal, agreed with CJ, is to end up as a **long-term equity partner** - the Davina McCall of men's health - with Ted's potentially becoming Voy's B2B arm on a revenue share. If today's call ends with everyone excited about a campaign, the frame has been set as **paid ambassador**, and every later conversation starts from there.

### Opener — say this

> "Max, Elisabeth, good to meet you both. Thanks for putting the work in on this.
>
> Before we get into the concepts - and I do want to see them - can I ask one framing question, because it will make my notes a lot more useful to you?"

Then:

> "Help me understand the shape you have in mind. Is Bear the face of a Voy campaign, or is he a partner whose own brand sits alongside yours? Those are two very different creative briefs and I want to give you notes against the right one."

**Good answer:** they talk about a joint brand, a co-owned proposition, something with Bear's name on it that persists. **Red flag:** they talk about talent, usage rights, campaign windows, and how many assets he'd need to shoot. If you hear "usage" or "deliverables", the frame is ambassador.

### Then let them present. Say very little.

While they present, listen for these four things and note them:
- Does the concept have a **Bear-branded product or pathway** inside it, or is Bear pointing at Voy's existing product?
- Whose **audience** does it run on? Voy's channels, Bear's, or both?
- Is there a **term** mentioned? Any number of months is a campaign, not a partnership.
- Does it look like the **Chatterjee** setup with a different face?

### After the presentation — the four questions, in this order

**1.**
> "That's genuinely strong work, and I can see the thinking. Tell me about the product side - in this concept, is there something with Bear's name on it, or is he speaking to Voy's existing TRT offer?"

*Good: a co-branded pathway or product. Red flag: "he's the voice of it."*

**2.**
> "And whose audience does this live on? Is this running on Voy's channels, or is the assumption that Bear's channels carry it too?"

*Ask because this is where the value transfer is. If Bear's channels carry it, Voy is buying reach and should be paying for reach, on top of anything else.*

**3.**
> "What's the time horizon you're imagining? Are we talking about a campaign, or something Bear is genuinely in for the long run?"

*Good: years, equity, "build something together." Red flag: a campaign window, a flight, a quarter.*

**4.**
> "Last one, and it's the important one. Bear isn't looking to be an ambassador for anyone at this stage of his career. What he's interested in is building something he part-owns and stays with. Is that a conversation Voy is open to having?"

*This is the whole call. Say it calmly, as information, not as a demand. Then stop talking and let them answer.*

### If they push on Ted's — the deflection

They may ask about Ted's subscribers, conversion, what's working, how many clinics. Say:

> "I'd rather not get into Ted's numbers today - that's a separate conversation, and it sits with CJ and Raemy. Let's keep this one on the creative and the shape of the partnership."

Then move on immediately. Do not soften it, do not give a partial number as a consolation. **Voy is a competitor in the same category and may be reading the market through this call.**

### The holding line — use this instead of a verdict

You will feel pressure to say whether you like the concepts. Do not give a live verdict. Say:

> "This is strong, and I don't want to give you off-the-cuff notes on work you've put real effort into. Send me the deck and I'll come back to you with proper written notes this week, once Bear has seen it too."

That does three useful things: it is flattering and true, it gets you the deck, and it means BGV's actual response is written, considered, and issued on your timing rather than improvised on theirs.

### Close — say this

> "Really appreciate you both walking us through it. Send the deck over and I'll come back with notes this week. And Max, on the bigger shape - worth Bear and CJ picking that up with you properly, because I think there's a version of this that's a lot more interesting than a campaign for both of us."

### Never say today

- Anything about **selling Ted's**, in any direction, even as a joke.
- Any **Ted's number** - subscribers, revenue, clinics, conversion.
- **"We love it, let's do it."**
- Any **date, shoot, or commitment** to deliverables.
- Any comment on how **little** Ted's is next to Voy. They know the size difference. Do not narrate it.

### What you need to know going in

- **Dream (dream.global)** is a group, not one company: **HelpBnk** (free community-sourced help for entrepreneurs), **Dream Media**, **Dream Ventures**, the **DreamFund** (pitch a dream in 60 seconds, win £5k from last month's profit), **DreamBrew**. Jack is CEO, Guy is CFO.

- Simon lives in **Tunbridge Wells** and offered to meet face to face. Worth knowing - he is local and this can easily become an in-person relationship.
- The brand alignment is genuinely strong. Squibb's mission is helping people build something; BGV's line is literally *Inspire. Equip. Empower.* This is not a stretch, which is exactly why it needs a defined shape rather than a warm handshake.

### The risk to hold in mind

With a partner this generous and this mission-led, the danger is never bad intent. It is that **everyone leaves delighted and nothing was actually agreed** - no entity, no equity, no term, no definition of what Bear is lending. Bear's name is the most valuable thing on the table and it is the easiest thing to give away in a good mood.

### Opener — say this

> "Simon, Jack, Guy - good to meet you all. Bear's genuinely taken with what you're building, he said as much on the thread, so we're coming at this warm.
>
> To make the hour useful: could you take us through what Dream actually is as a group, and then be really specific about where you see Bear in it?"

### The five questions

**1.**
> "What's the ask, specifically? Is this Bear's name, his time, his audience, or his capital? It could be any of them and we'd look at each differently."

*This is the single most valuable question of the call. Ask it early and let it sit.*

**2.**
> "Which entity in the group is this? You've got HelpBnk, Dream Ventures, the DreamFund, Dream Media - if Bear comes in, what is he coming into, and what does he own of it?"

*Good: a clear entity and a real equity conversation. Red flag: "we'll figure the structure out later, let's just start creating."*

**3.**
> "What does success look like twelve months from now? Give me the numbers you'd hold yourselves to."

*Squibb's world runs on reach and goodwill, which are real but hard to bank. This question turns it into something you can hold.*

**4.**
> "On the name - what is DreamBank, actually? Is it a fund, a platform, a content brand? I ask because 'bank' is a loaded word in the UK, and if Bear's name is going on something that looks to the public like it holds or promises money, we'd want to be sure of the regulatory position first."

*Say this once, lightly, then move on. Note they already spell it HelpBnk without the vowel, which suggests they have thought about exactly this. If they answer confidently, good - it means they have taken advice. If the answer is vague, that is a real item for Raemy and Helen, not a reason to cool on the deal.*

**5.**
> "And what do you want from Bear personally, versus from BGV as a business? Because his time is the constrained thing, and we protect it hard."

### Close — say this

> "This is really aligned with what Bear cares about, so the appetite is there from our side. What would help us is a one-pager: the entity, what you're proposing Bear puts in, what he gets, and over what period. Send that across and we'll come back to you quickly.
>
> And Simon - you're in Tunbridge Wells, we should do the next one in person."

**Do not commit today to:** a filming date, an appearance, a DreamFund judging role, an investment, or use of Bear's name in any announcement. Everything can be "send us the one-pager and we'll come back quickly." That is not a brush-off with people this warm, it is normal.
