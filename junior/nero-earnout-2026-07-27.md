## The swing, in one line

**Tyler wrote performance as a discount. We write it as upside.**

His structure is 3% base plus 2% at risk. Five is the ceiling, and Bear earns his way *down* to it.
Every performance clause in it can only ever subtract.

Ours makes 8% the floor and performance strictly additive on top, up to 15%. Same word, opposite
direction. And it lets you hold the fifteen you already asked for without re-anchoring, because now
he only pays the top of the range out of results he has already banked.

He cannot object to the principle. He introduced it.

## The five changes that move the money

### 1. Blended CAC, not Meta cost per action

This is the most valuable line in the document and he may not see it immediately.

**Meta cost per action** only pays Bear for making Meta ads cheaper. **Blended CAC** is total media
spend divided by total orders, so every order Bear generates that never touches an ad **cuts the
blended number mechanically**, even with Meta CAC flat. That is precisely the halo you argued for on
20 July, converted from an argument into a metric.

It is also the honest measure of the thing he actually needs. Expect him to fight it once he works
out what it does. The defence is that he chose it: he says Nero's $12M is grounded in real
performance, so the total cost of getting a customer is the number that matters, not one channel's
dashboard.

### 2. Baseline locked at $64.38, not $60.37

He sent both. **$60.37 is the trailing twelve months, $64.38 is the trailing six.** Every reduction is
measured from the baseline, so a higher baseline is worth real money to us, and the six-month figure
is also the truer picture of where the business actually is.

He will push for the twelve-month number. Hold the six: it is the current run rate, and it is his own
screenshot with a date on it.

*Caveat to know before the call: $64.38 is Meta cost per action, not verified blended CAC. If real
blended is materially lower once he opens the books, our baseline drops on true-up. Write the true-up
into the term sheet at 30 days so it happens in daylight rather than as an argument later. Also worth
one casual question: is "action" a purchase, or an earlier event.*

### 3. A cash line that is self-funding

Equity is the half he controls and can dilute, delay or cancel. Add one cash line and make it pay out
of money he would otherwise have spent: **Bear takes a share of the media saving he creates**, measured
on the same blended number.

At his own year-two forecast, $8M of revenue at a $100 order value, so 80,000 orders:

| Blended CAC | Media spend | Saved vs $64.38 baseline | Bear at 20% | at 25% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $64.38 (baseline) | $5,150,000 | - | - | - |
| $60 | $4,800,000 | $350,000 | $70,000 | $88,000 |
| $55 | $4,400,000 | $750,000 | $150,000 | $188,000 |
| **$50** | **$4,000,000** | **$1,150,000** | **$230,000** | **$288,000** |
| $45 | $3,600,000 | $1,550,000 | $310,000 | $388,000 |

The framing that makes this easy for him to accept: **he is paying out of money he did not spend.**
It costs him nothing he currently has. And it is the cheapest financing available to him, because to
double to $8M at today's CAC he needs $5.15M of media out of a bootstrapped balance sheet with no debt
and no outside capital. At $50 blended he needs $4.0M. That $1.15M gap is roughly the difference
between the doubling being fundable and not.

Ask 25%, land at 20%.

### 4. Tranches, with a one-way ratchet

1% of equity per sustained reduction step, earned in 1% increments, each requiring:

- the reduction held across **two consecutive quarters**, not a single good month, and
- **revenue flat or up** in that period.

**Volunteer the revenue condition yourself.** It stops him paying Bear for a shrinking business, which
is the obvious hole he would otherwise find, and raising it first costs us nothing and buys the
credibility to hold the harder points. It is the cheapest good faith in the deal.

**Once a tranche is earned it is earned.** No clawback, no re-test. His six-month trial window is a
clawback dressed as a trial. Kill it, or it works both ways and the rights revert to us if we use it.

### 5. Deliverables scale with the tranches

Do not hand over 31 deliverables a year up front against paper that can be cancelled. Base tier at
signature, higher tiers unlock as tranches unlock. Bear does more when it is working.

This is also the right thing to trade. **Give ground on deliverables to hold the percentage**, never
the reverse. Deliverables are the cheapest concession available and he has told us he values them.
