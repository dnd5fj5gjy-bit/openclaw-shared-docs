Three things worth watching when you transcribe them.

The formula column and the label claim column are not the same number for two ingredients. Calcium goes in as 400mg of Aquamin-F marine algae but the label claim is 120mg of calcium. Acerola cherry extract goes in as 360mg but the label claim is 90mg of vitamin C. The website should show the label claim figures, not the input amounts. Same applies to vitamin K2, which is 30mg of the MK-7 ingredient delivering 60mcg of actual K2.

The panels are in US format with percent Daily Value against a 2,000 calorie diet. That is fine as a reference but it is not the UK nutrition declaration format, so do not put the percent DV column on a UK-facing page as is.

Mini Savage contains milk, from the bovine colostrum. The allergen line needs to be on that product page. It also has no lung and no testes, unlike the adult blend, so the organ list is genuinely different between the two.

The formula was locked in late June after Jesse and Bear signed off on the switch to maple sugar, so anything on the site referencing monk fruit or any other sweetener is out of date and should come off.
