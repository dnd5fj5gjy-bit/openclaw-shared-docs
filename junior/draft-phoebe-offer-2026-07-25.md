## Two things for you before this goes out

**1. The profit test needs one line or it defeats itself.** You have said the rise happens when the second order is placed and the business is making a profit. If the cost of that second stock order is itself counted against profit, the test can never be met at the moment it is measured, because you are placing a large order on the same day. The agreement should say profit is measured on trading to that date, excluding the cost of the order being placed. That is not me reopening the test, it is making sure the test you chose can actually be passed.

I have drafted it as "trading profitably at that date". Tell me if you want it tighter than that and I will define it as cumulative revenue since launch exceeding cumulative costs, excluding your own investment and excluding the order being placed.

**2. Still unanswered from earlier, and it should not hold up the email but must be settled before anything is signed.** Which entity contracts her, given she is in Devon and Modern Savage Inc is a Delaware C-Corp. That drives how she invoices, and it drives whether the equity conversation in phase two has a sensible answer. That is Raemy.

The consultancy agreement itself should be papered by Ignition rather than by me, because the employment-status question in the UK is the sort of thing that is cheap to get right now and expensive to get wrong later.
