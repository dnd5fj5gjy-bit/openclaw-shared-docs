## 2. Affiliate and discount are two different things

This is the part worth separating, because it dissolves most of the problem.

A discount code is not the payment, it is the **tracking**. It became the default because it is the only
attribution device that survives being spoken aloud in a video. The commission is paid on the sale, and it
can be paid just as easily on a **tracked link with no discount attached**.

So "no discounts" does not mean "no affiliates". It means replacing three jobs the code was quietly doing:

| The code was doing | Replace it with |
|---|---|
| Telling us the sale came from Justin | Unique link, vanity URL `modernsavage.co/justin`, plus a post-purchase "how did you hear about us" to catch spoken mentions |
| Giving the customer a reason to act now | Early position in the pre-order queue, or a bundled extra. Not money off |
| Giving Justin a reason to post | A fee, a licence, a rev share, or something creative he actually wants. See section 4 |

**Worth checking before building anything:** there is a "Bear Grylls / Adventure Garage Affiliate Setup"
thread in your mailbox from 25 Jun 2026 (Will Gardenswartz, Jonathan Mathes). BGV may already have
affiliate rails we can reuse rather than stand up from scratch. I have not opened it yet.

## 3. Your instinct is right, and it is worth saying why

Not a constraint to work around. Three reasons a code would actively hurt right now:

1. **The pre-order book paid full price and has not received anything.** A public code before a single
   customer has taken delivery tells them they overpaid, at exactly the moment we may also need to send
   them a delay notice on the October date.
2. **It sets the anchor before the brand has one.** Pre-launch is the only time you get to establish what
   the product is worth. A launch discount is a price cut you never take back.
3. **It trains the audience to wait.** Every code teaches the list that full price is for people who were
   not paying attention.

Pricing parity across the three SKUs is already an open question with you (item 7 of the twelve). Adding a
discount layer on top of an unresolved price ladder makes both harder.

**It also inverts the relationship.** Instead of a creator promoting a supplement to a mismatched audience,
