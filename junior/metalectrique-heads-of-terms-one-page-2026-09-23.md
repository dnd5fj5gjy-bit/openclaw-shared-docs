# Heads of Terms: Defence Exclusivity

**Metalectrique aluminium-air technology**

**Date:** [ ] September 2026

**Between:** **Edward Michael Bear Grylls** and **Jesse Grylls** ("**Grylls**"), who may pass this agreement to a company they set up; **MAL Research and Development Limited**, company number 07964862, trading as Metalectrique ("**Metalectrique**"); and **Trevor Jackson** personally.

**These heads of terms are legally binding on signature.**

---

**1. Defence exclusivity.** Metalectrique gives Grylls the exclusive worldwide right to make, have made and sell products using its aluminium-air technology for **defence and military use**. Defence and military use means anything supplied to armed forces, a ministry of defence, a defence or security agency or a defence contractor, including drones and other unmanned systems. Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not sell, license or supply the technology for defence use to anyone else, or help anyone else to. Everything outside defence stays with Metalectrique.

**2. Existing contacts.** Metalectrique's currently active defence contacts, **Saab / BlueBear, Swift Aviation and QinetiQ**, are included in Grylls's exclusivity. Metalectrique will take them forward through Grylls.

**3. Royalty.** Grylls will pay Metalectrique a royalty of **[5]%** of Grylls's net sales of defence products, and **[7.5]%** on sales to the contacts in clause 2, paid quarterly.

**4. Manufacturing.** Grylls chooses who manufactures defence products. If Grylls chooses Metalectrique, it is paid an agreed manufacturing price as well as the royalty.

**5. Non-circumvention.** For three years, Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not deal with anyone Grylls introduces, on defence work, except through Grylls. If they do, Metalectrique will pay Grylls what Grylls would have earned on that deal.

**6. Confidentiality.** Each side keeps the other's information, including contacts introduced, confidential for five years, and Metalectrique's trade secrets for as long as they remain secret. This replaces any separate non-disclosure agreement.

**7. Names.** Metalectrique will not use the Grylls name in any material without Grylls's written approval.

**8. How long it lasts.** The exclusivity continues for as long as Grylls is actively working with the technology. If twelve months pass with no introductions, demonstrations, quotes, trials or sales by Grylls, Metalectrique may end it on written notice. Grylls may end it on 30 days' notice. Clauses 5 and 6 survive either way.

**9. Next step.** A fuller licence and supply contract on these terms will follow within 90 days. Until it is signed, these heads of terms apply. English law applies. Each side pays its own costs.
