## Channel - the comparator matters

Electral and Enerzal are FDC brands. FDC is a **pharmaceutical** company and Electral's
distribution is chemist-led. BGEP's plan is kirana, street vendor, petrol pump - **general
trade, not pharmacy**. So Electral is the right benchmark for CATEGORY SIZE and for
per-outlet RATE OF SALE, but it is not evidence that our channel will carry us.

The right channel analogue is **Glucon-D** (Zydus Wellness), the mass glucose powder brand
sold through kirana, which holds 60.4% of the glucose powder category (up 203bps) and has
launched a 20 g sachet to drive penetration in exactly the low-price segment we are entering.

## Trade margin norms (India FMCG, general trade)

| Link | Published norm | Our model assumes |
|---|---|---|
| Retailer (kirana), packaged FMCG | 8-15% | **15%** (top of band) |
| Distributor | 4-8% typical; 10-12% cited for some categories | **8%** (top of band) |
| New/regional brands, to buy shelf | 15-20% retailer | we are at 15% |

Reading: our assumed margins are at the TOP of the normal band, which is right for buying
shelf space as an unknown brand - but it means there is no headroom left. If a distributor
demands more, it comes straight out of the Rs 2.10 contribution.
