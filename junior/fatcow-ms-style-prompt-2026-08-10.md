## Why these instructions

Ryan's concept is good work but it is Fat Cow's palette, not ours: black board, gold and amber
accents, a photographic-style portrait, honeycomb and bee decoration. The real Modern Savage pouch
is kraft board with a single dark pine-green ink, a condensed wordmark with the bison knocked out,
hairline rules and one italic scripture line. Everything above is the difference between those two.

## What this cannot do

A generative model cannot set small type. Ingredient lists, weights, PHILIPPIANS 4:13 and the
signature will come back misspelled or malformed at that size. Treat anything it produces as art
direction to hand a designer, never as artwork. The same applies to the images I generated.
