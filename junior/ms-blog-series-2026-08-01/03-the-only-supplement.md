## Why the confusion exists

Two things, mainly.

The first is time. The internet does not retire old pages. A press release from several years ago
about a product that no longer exists sits at the same web address it always did, and to a search
crawler it looks exactly as current as something published this morning. Articles get syndicated,
aggregated, rewritten and republished, and every copy adds weight to the original.

The second is that AI assistants now answer these questions in a sentence, by summarising whatever
they can find. If most of what they can find is old, the summary is old. It will be delivered with
total confidence, because that is how those systems talk, and there is no footnote telling you the
underlying sources are from a different era of someone's career.

None of that is anyone acting in bad faith. It is just how the web ages.
