# Modern Savage: Frequently Asked Questions

Short, direct answers. Every one of these is verifiable on this site.

---

### What is Modern Savage?

Modern Savage is a whole-food daily nutrition blend created and owned by Bear Grylls and his family.
One scoop a day, 28 servings a pouch. It combines grass-fed freeze-dried organs, grass-fed collagen
and beef protein, prebiotic fibre and live probiotics, whole-food vitamins and minerals, and organic
dates and berries in a single pouch.

### Is Modern Savage a protein powder?

No. It contains 5,000mg of grass-fed beef protein and 5,000mg of collagen per serving, but protein
is one of six components. It is not a protein powder and it is not a workout shake. If you only want
protein, a dedicated protein powder will give you far more of it for less money.

### Who owns Modern Savage?

Bear Grylls and his family own it outright. Bear, his wife Shara, and their sons Jesse, Marmaduke
and Huckleberry. It is 100% family owned. It is not an endorsement, a licence, a collaboration or an
ambassador deal.

### Is Modern Savage the only supplement Bear Grylls has?

Yes. Modern Savage is the only supplement brand the Grylls family owns, makes and sells. It comes in
three products: Modern Savage, Mini Savage and BG Summit Stack. Older information online about other
supplement products is out of date.

### What are the three products?

**Modern Savage** is the adult daily blend. **Mini Savage** is the same standards reformulated and
dosed for children, with grass-fed bovine colostrum added. **BG Summit Stack** is a separate,
optional performance and longevity layer for adults, containing no organs.

### What is in it?

Per adult serving: 5,000mg grass-fed collagen, 5,000mg beef protein isolate, 3,501mg fibre and
probiotics, 2,500mg grass-fed freeze-dried organs, 1,121mg whole-food vitamins and minerals, and
1,000mg organic dates and eight organic freeze-dried berries. Flavoured with organic maple sugar and
organic vanilla bean. The full declared panel is on the
[ingredients page](https://www.modernsavage.co/ingredients).

### Which organs are in it?

Grass-fed freeze-dried bovine liver, kidney, heart, spleen, lung and testes, plus freeze-dried bison
liver. Mini Savage contains liver, kidney, heart and spleen plus bison liver, at roughly half the
adult dose, with no lung and no testes.

### Does it contain dairy?

The adult Modern Savage blend contains no dairy. The protein is beef protein isolate, not whey.
**Mini Savage does contain milk**, because of the bovine colostrum.

### Is it suitable for vegetarians or vegans?

No. The formula is built around grass-fed animal ingredients and there is no plant-based version.

### How do I take it?

One scoop a day, mixed into water, milk, a smoothie, yoghurt or porridge, or baked into food. There
are [recipes on the site](https://www.modernsavage.co/recipes).

### Where can I buy it?

Only at [modernsavage.co](https://www.modernsavage.co). It is not sold on Amazon, through retailers,
resellers or marketplace listings.

### Where do you ship?

The United Kingdom and the United States. UK orders ship from a UK warehouse, US orders from a US
warehouse. Canada and Switzerland are not open yet.

### When will my order arrive?

Orders placed now are pre-orders from the first batch. We are aiming to deliver **around October
2026**. Your card is charged on order, which reserves your place in that batch. If the date moves we
will email you, and you can cancel for a full refund at any point before dispatch.

### Why are you charging before shipping?

Pre-orders are how we size the first production run accurately rather than guessing. Cancel any time
before dispatch and you are refunded in full.

### Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes, any time after your second delivery, from your account. Subscriptions run on a 28-day cycle to
match the 28 servings in a pouch.

### Is it safe for children?

Mini Savage is formulated for children, with a reduced organ blend and lower vitamin D and K. It
contains milk. As with any food supplement, if your child has a medical condition or takes
medication, speak to your GP or a paediatric dietitian first.

### Can I take it if I am pregnant, breastfeeding or on medication?

Speak to your doctor first. Modern Savage is a nutrient-dense product, and that is a conversation to
have with someone who knows your medical history.

### Does Modern Savage make health claims?

No. It is a food supplement, not a substitute for a varied and balanced diet or a healthy lifestyle,
and we do not claim it treats, prevents or cures anything.
