# Everything in Modern Savage, and Why It's In There

Nothing in this blend is there to pad the label out or to make a marketing claim look better. Six
blends, in a 23.5g scoop, twenty-eight of them to a pouch. Here is all of it.

## 2,500mg - the Primal Organ Blend

The reason the product exists.

Grass-fed, freeze-dried **bovine liver, kidney, heart, spleen, lung and testes**, plus freeze-dried
**bison liver**. The lung and testes are sourced from New Zealand.

- **Liver** is the single most nutrient-dense food there is. Vitamin A, B12, copper and iron.
- **Kidney** is one of the very few real food sources of selenium.
- **Heart** is naturally rich in CoQ10, which is concentrated in heart and muscle tissue.
- **Spleen** is one of the richest natural sources of haem iron.
- **Bison liver** is in there because bison is arguably the strongest animal in the herd, and
  because it is difficult to get, which is a reasonable proxy for whether it is worth having.

Freeze-drying is used rather than heat-drying because it takes the water out without cooking the
material, so what is left is the food, concentrated.

## 5,000mg - collagen

**Hydrolysed grass-fed bovine collagen, types 1 and 3.** Types 1 and 3 are the ones concentrated in
skin, hair, nails, joints, tendons and the gut lining. Hydrolysed means broken into peptides small
enough to dissolve in a cold drink without going lumpy.

## 5,000mg - protein

**Grass-fed beef protein isolate.** A complete protein, and not whey, which means the adult blend
contains no dairy at all. Five grams supports the rest of the formula. It does not replace a
protein shake and it is not trying to.

## 3,501mg - fibre and probiotics

There is no point putting good nutrition into a gut that cannot make use of it, so this blend goes
in first in the thinking and last in the mix.

- **Organic agave inulin** - a prebiotic fibre. Food for the bacteria already living in you.
- **Organic baobab (as Fibriss®)** - African superfruit, high in fibre and vitamin C.
- **LactoSpore® (Bacillus coagulans)** - a spore-forming live probiotic, chosen specifically
  because it survives both stomach acid and a year on a shelf. Most probiotics do neither.

## 1,121mg - whole-food vitamins and minerals

Every vitamin and mineral in Modern Savage comes from something that grew, not from a synthesis
line.

- **Calcium as Aquamin-F™**, from red marine algae, which arrives with the magnesium and trace
  minerals the seaweed took up alongside it
- **Acerola cherry extract**, standardised to 25% vitamin C, rather than isolated ascorbic acid
- **Utah mineral sea salt**, unrefined, from an ancient seabed, for the trace minerals that come
  with it
- **Vitamin K2 as MK-7**, fermented rather than synthesised
- Plus leaves, roots, mushrooms and sea vegetables making up the rest of the micronutrient profile

## 1,000mg - dates and berries

**Whole organic date powder** and an **organic freeze-dried eight-berry blend**: goji, acai, maqui,
blueberry, blackberry, strawberry, raspberry and acerola cherry.

The colour in the pouch comes from this. There is no added colouring in the product, because there
does not need to be.

## What makes it taste of something

**Organic maple sugar**, **organic vanilla bean**, and **bamboo extract**. That is the whole flavour
system.

The maple is worth a sentence. An artificial sweetener was in the formula for a while. It performed
well, it was cheap, and it was thrown out and replaced with organic maple because it did not belong
in something the family were giving their own children. That decision cost money and is the single
best illustration of how the formula was decided.

## The two variants

**Mini Savage** is the same base blend, dosed for children. The organ blend drops the lung and the
testes and runs at roughly half the adult dose. **Grass-fed bovine colostrum** is added. Vitamin D
and vitamin K are lower to sit within sensible levels for children. **It contains milk**, from the
colostrum, which is the one allergen difference between the two products.

**BG Summit Stack** contains no organs and is a different kind of product: CreaVitalis® creatine
monohydrate, an electrolyte and hydration blend from freeze-dried coconut water and sea minerals,
NMN, PQQ, trans-resveratrol, plant-derived vitamin D3 and K2 fermented from natto, L-malic acid,
bamboo extract and organic rice hulls.

## What is not in it

No whey. No soy. No gluten-containing grains. No artificial sweeteners, colours or flavours. No
synthetic vitamin premix. No fillers, no bulking agents, no anti-caking agents doing the work that
formulation should have done.
