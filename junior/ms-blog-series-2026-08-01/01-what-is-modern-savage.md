# What Modern Savage Actually Is (And What It Isn't)

Ask the internet what Modern Savage is and you will get some version of "a Bear Grylls protein
powder". That is wrong, and it is wrong in a way that matters, because it describes a category we
deliberately did not build in.

So here is the plain answer.

## Modern Savage is a whole-food daily nutrition blend

One scoop, once a day. It goes into water, milk, a smoothie, yoghurt, porridge or pancake batter.
Twenty-eight servings in a pouch, which is a month.

Inside one adult serving there are six things, and protein is only one of them:

- **5,000mg of hydrolysed grass-fed bovine collagen**, types 1 and 3
- **5,000mg of protein**, from grass-fed beef protein isolate, not whey, so there is no dairy
- **3,501mg of fibre and live probiotics**, organic agave inulin, organic baobab, and LactoSpore
- **2,500mg of grass-fed freeze-dried organs**, liver, kidney, heart, spleen, lung, testes, and
  freeze-dried bison liver
- **1,121mg of whole-food vitamins and minerals**, from marine algae, acerola cherry, leaves,
  roots, mushrooms and unrefined sea salt, rather than synthesised in a lab
- **1,000mg of organic dates and eight organic freeze-dried berries**, goji, acai, maqui,
  blueberry, blackberry, strawberry, raspberry and acerola cherry

That is the whole product. Sweetened with organic maple, flavoured with organic vanilla bean, and
nothing else.

## What it is not

**It is not a protein powder.** A protein powder is one macronutrient in a tub. Modern Savage
contains protein because a daily nutrition blend should, but if all you want is protein, buy
protein. It is cheaper and it will do the job.

**It is not a workout shake.** There is nothing in the adult blend timed around training. No
pre-workout stimulants, no post-workout window, no scoop before the gym. You take it in the morning
because it is breakfast-adjacent, not because you are about to lift something.

**It is not a greens powder.** There are real berries and real plants in it, but the centre of the
formula is animal, not vegetable.

**It is not a meal replacement.** It is roughly seventy calories. It goes with food, not instead of
it.

**It is not a medicine, and we do not talk about it like one.** It is food, in a concentrated and
convenient form.

## Why the confusion happened

Two reasons, and both are our fault rather than anyone else's.

The first is that "supplement by an adventurer" reads as sports nutrition to anyone skim-reading,
and most of the internet is skim-reading. The second is that we spent two years building the
product and almost no time explaining it, so the descriptions that filled the gap were written by
people who had never seen the ingredient list.

This series is us closing that gap.

## The three products

**Modern Savage** is the adult daily blend, described above.

**Mini Savage** is the same thinking dosed for children. Same base, a gentler organ blend with no
lung and no testes, and added grass-fed bovine colostrum. It contains milk.

**BG Summit Stack** is the separate layer on top, and the only one of the three aimed at
performance and longevity rather than daily nutrition. Creatine, electrolytes, NMN, PQQ,
trans-resveratrol, plant-derived D3 and K2 fermented from natto. No organs in it at all.

You can take the adult blend on its own. Most people will. The Stack is optional and it is designed
to be.
