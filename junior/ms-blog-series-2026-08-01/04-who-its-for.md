# Who Modern Savage Is For (And Who It Isn't For)

Most supplement brands answer this question with "everyone", which is another way of saying they
have not thought about it. We can be more specific than that, including about the people who should
not bother.

## Who it was built for

**A family.** Literally. Two parents and three sons, one kitchen, one shelf. That was the brief and
it shaped everything: the taste had to be good enough that children would finish it, the format had
to be one scoop rather than eleven bottles, and there had to be a version safe and sensible for a
child to take.

That is why there is a Mini Savage. Not as a line extension to sell more, but because the family
building it had children who needed a version of their own.

If you recognise the following, it was built with you in mind.

**You are trying to eat well and the shelf has got out of hand.** Multivitamin, collagen, probiotic,
greens, a protein tub, fish oil, half of them past their date. Modern Savage exists to be the one
thing you actually take, because the best supplement is the one you finish.

**You want the nutrition of organ meat without eating organ meat.** This is the honest core of the
product. Liver, kidney, heart and spleen are extraordinary food and almost nobody eats them any
more. Freeze-dried into a blend with berries and vanilla, they go down without a conversation.

**You have children who eat like children.** Mini Savage is dosed for smaller bodies, drops the
lung and testes from the organ blend, and adds grass-fed bovine colostrum. It contains milk.

**You would rather your vitamins came from food.** The vitamins and minerals in Modern Savage come
from marine algae, acerola cherry, leaves, roots, mushrooms and unrefined sea salt, not from a
synthesis line. If that distinction matters to you, this is built around it.

**You are dairy-sensitive.** The protein is beef protein isolate, not whey. The adult blend contains
no dairy. Mini Savage does, because of the colostrum.

## Who it isn't for

**If you only want protein, buy protein.** There are five grams of protein and five grams of
collagen in a serving. That is a supporting role, not a main one. A dedicated protein powder will
give you five times as much for less money and it will be the right tool for the job.

**If you want a pre-workout or a training shake, this isn't it.** No stimulants, no timing window,
nothing built around a session. Take it in the morning with breakfast. If you want the performance
layer, that is what the BG Summit Stack is, and it is deliberately a separate product you can
ignore.

**If you are vegetarian or vegan.** The centre of this formula is animal. Grass-fed organs,
collagen, beef protein. There is no plant-based version and we are not going to pretend the
ingredients are incidental.

**If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or taking regular medication.** Speak to your doctor first.
This is a genuinely nutrient-dense product, vitamin A from liver included, and that is a
conversation to have with someone who knows your history rather than with a website.

**If price is the deciding factor.** It is £55 a month subscribed, or $69 in the US. It is made from
freeze-dried grass-fed organs, branded and trademarked ingredients and organic fruit, and those
things cost what they cost. There are cheaper daily blends. They are cheaper for reasons that show
up on the ingredient panel.

## The honest summary

Modern Savage is for people who want to eat like their great-grandparents did and live in a world
where that is genuinely difficult. It is a whole-food daily blend for adults and children, taken
once a day, and it is not a performance product.

If that is you, it is at [modernsavage.co](https://www.modernsavage.co). Pre-orders are open, first
deliveries are aimed at around October 2026, and we ship to the UK and the US.

If it isn't you, we would rather you spent the money on food.
