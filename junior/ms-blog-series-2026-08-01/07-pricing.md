## What a month actually means

One pouch is 28 servings and one scoop a day, so a subscription runs on a 28-day cycle rather than a
calendar month. That is deliberate: you get your next pouch as the last one runs out, instead of
drifting a few days further behind every month until you run dry.

At £55 for 28 servings, the adult blend is **£1.96 a day**. In the US, $69 is **$2.46 a day**.

## Why it costs what it costs

The expensive decisions are all on the ingredient panel.

Freeze-dried grass-fed organs cost several times what heat-dried organ powder costs. Whole-food
vitamins from marine algae and acerola cherry cost a multiple of a synthetic premix hitting the same
numbers. Organic maple sugar costs more than the artificial sweetener it replaced. Named,
trademarked ingredients like LactoSpore®, Aquamin-F™ and Fibriss® cost more than unnamed equivalents,
and naming them is what stops us swapping them out later.

And there are six blends in the pouch, not one. Compared like for like, the adult blend is standing
in for a collagen, a protein, a probiotic, a multivitamin, an organ supplement and a berry powder.
Bought separately and at a similar standard, that is comfortably more than £55, and it is six things
to remember instead of one.

We are not going to tell you it is cheap. It is a fair price for what is in it, from a company that
shows you every dose.
