# BG Summit Stack: The Layer on Top, and Why It's a Separate Product

Of the three Modern Savage products, this is the one that most people should probably ignore, and we
would rather say that than sell it to someone who does not want it.

## What it is

A separate 28-serving pouch, taken alongside the daily blend rather than instead of it. It contains
**no organs at all**, which makes it a different kind of product from the other two.

In it:

- **CreaVitalis® creatine monohydrate** - the most studied supplement ingredient there is, and the
  one with the least mystique attached to it
- **An electrolyte and hydration blend** - freeze-dried coconut water powder, seawater minerals and
  sea salt
- **NMN** (nicotinamide mononucleotide)
- **PQQ** (pyrroloquinoline quinone)
- **Trans-resveratrol**
- **Plant-derived vitamin D3, and vitamin K2 as MK-7 fermented from natto** - D3 and K2 are paired
  because they work on the same system and are usually taken together
- **L-malic acid**, **bamboo extract** and **organic rice hulls**

Same standards as the rest of the range: named trademarked ingredients where the specific form
matters, no artificial sweeteners, no fillers doing the work formulation should have done.

## Why it is separate

Because the daily blend is for everyone in the house and this is not.

Modern Savage and Mini Savage are food, concentrated. They are built to be taken every day by an
adult or a child without anyone thinking about it. The Summit Stack is a deliberate choice by an
adult who wants a specific set of compounds, and it did not belong in a family blend.

Three practical reasons it is its own pouch:

**It is adults only.** Several of these ingredients are formulated and studied for adults. They have
no place in a children's product, and if the Stack were folded into the daily blend there could not
be a children's version at all.

**Not everyone wants it.** Most people taking Modern Savage want the nutrition of nose-to-tail
eating in a form they will actually swallow. That is the whole product for them, and adding
creatine and NMN to it would mean charging them for something they did not ask for.

**It lets you change your mind.** You can start the Stack, stop the Stack, or run it seasonally,
without touching the thing you take every day.

## Who it is actually for

Adults who are already training, already eating well, already taking the daily blend, and who want
the layer that sits above all of that. If that is not a description of your week yet, the daily
blend is where the return is, and the Stack will still be there later.

Creatine in particular is worth being unromantic about. It is cheap, extremely well evidenced, and
does not need a story attached to it. It is in here because it works, not because it sounds
exotic.

## Taking it

One scoop a day, 28 servings a pouch, alongside your daily blend rather than in place of it. It
mixes into water or a smoothie.
