# Mini Savage: Why We Built a Separate Formula for Children

The easy version of a children's supplement is the adult one with a smaller scoop and a cartoon on
the front. We did not want that in our own kitchen, so we did not make it.

Mini Savage is a separate formula. Here is what is different and why.

## The organ blend is rebuilt, not reduced

The adult Primal Organ Blend is 2,500mg of grass-fed freeze-dried bovine liver, kidney, heart,
spleen, lung and testes, plus bison liver.

**Mini Savage runs at roughly half that dose, and drops the lung and the testes entirely.** What is
left is liver, kidney, heart and spleen, plus bison liver. That is the nutrient-dense core of
nose-to-tail eating with the parts that do not belong in a child's daily blend taken out.

## Colostrum is added

**Grass-fed bovine colostrum** goes into Mini Savage and is not in the adult blend. Colostrum is the
first milk a cow produces after calving, and it is the one ingredient in the range chosen
specifically for children rather than scaled down for them.

**This means Mini Savage contains milk.** It is the only allergen difference between the two
products, and if there is a dairy allergy in your house, this is the fact that matters most on the
page.

## Vitamin D and vitamin K are lower

Children are not small adults, and the fat-soluble vitamins are where that stops being a slogan.
Vitamin D and vitamin K are both dosed down in Mini Savage to sit within sensible levels for a
child.

This is the least visible difference between the two products and the one that took the most work.

## Everything else is the same standard

Same grass-fed collagen and beef protein. Same prebiotic fibre and LactoSpore® probiotic. Same
whole-food vitamins and minerals from marine algae, acerola cherry and unrefined sea salt. Same
organic dates and eight organic freeze-dried berries. Same organic maple and vanilla, no artificial
sweeteners, no colours, no fillers.

The standards did not get relaxed for the children's version. They got tighter.

## Why it exists at all

Because the family building Modern Savage had three sons, and the whole problem they were trying to
solve was that children will not eat organ meat.

That is not a criticism of children. Adults will not eat it either. Heart, lung and liver are a hard
sell at any age, and the result is that the most nutrient-dense food available to us has quietly
disappeared from the way most families eat.

Freeze-dried, blended with real berries and organic maple, and stirred into a smoothie or yoghurt,
it goes down without a negotiation. That is the entire trick, and it is why there is a children's
version rather than a note on the adult pack saying "half a scoop for kids".

## How to use it

One scoop a day, 28 servings a pouch. It works in milk, a smoothie, yoghurt, porridge, or baked
into pancakes, which in practice is how most children will actually take it. There are
[recipes on the site](https://www.modernsavage.co/recipes).

## The honest caveats

- **It contains milk.** Colostrum. Do not give it to a child with a dairy allergy.
- **It is a food supplement, not a substitute for a varied and balanced diet.** No powder makes up
  for what is on the plate.
- **If your child has a medical condition or takes any medication, speak to your GP or a paediatric
  dietitian first.** This is a nutrient-dense product and that is a conversation worth having with
  someone who knows their history.
