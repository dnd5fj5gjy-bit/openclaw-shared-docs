## 2. His counter is a smaller offer than his opening. That is the headline.

Put them side by side.

| | 6 July opening | 23 July "counter" |
|---|---|---|
| Equity | 5% | 5% headline, but **3% base + 2% undefined performance earn-out** |
| Vesting | 50% on signing, 25% Y1, 25% Y2 | Vesting schedule **plus a 6-month trial window** |
| Royalty | **10% of net revenue** on a Bear range | **Gone. Not mentioned.** |
| Bear's obligations | "creative deliverables", unspecified | 24 posts/stories, 4 quarterly ad shoots, 2 podcast/media mentions, 1 pop-up appearance, **per year** |
| Rights | Full ad whitelisting | Paid ad, whitelisting, social **and website** usage rights |

He cut guaranteed equity from 5% to 3%, deleted the royalty entirely, added a six-month window in
which he can walk, and added roughly thirty Bear deliverables a year that did not exist before.
Presented as holding firm against a 15% ask, it reads as a concession. It is the opposite.

**Price what he is asking for.** 3% of $12M is $360k of unlisted minority ordinary shares granted for
services, no information rights, no put, cancellable at six months. Even taking it at face value over
a two-year schedule that is $180k a year against about 31 deliverables plus full commercial usage.
Roughly **$5,800 a deliverable**, and four of those are whole shoot days. Discount the paper for
illiquidity and minority position at the normal 40-60% and it is $2,300 to $3,500.

That is the line to use, because it is arithmetic and not opinion: *below our rate for a single post.*

Two smaller things worth striking: **"2 podcast/media mentions" cannot be contractually promised** -
that is third-party editorial, it has to be best efforts or it comes out. And **website usage rights**
were not in the July offer and are the most permanent thing on the list.

Even at a generous 70% gross margin and before any overhead, warehousing, salary or write-down, a

**Do not use this as an attack. Use it as the reason Bear is worth more than 5%.** The business is not
bad. It is a company whose growth is entirely rented and whose rent went up 6.6% in six months. His own
forecast makes it sharper: doubling to $8M at a $60 CAC and a $100 AOV means roughly **80,000 orders
and $4.8M of Meta spend**, out of a bootstrapped, undiluted balance sheet with no debt. That money has
to come from somewhere, or the doubling does not happen.

Bear is the only line in the plan that produces revenue not carrying the $60 toll. That is the whole
commercial case, and it is his data that makes it.

**Three questions that sharpen it, ask them casually:** is "per action" cost per purchase or an earlier
event; what percentage of revenue is returning customers; and what is blended CAC across all orders
rather than paid-attributed. If a real repeat rate exists, the picture improves and he will want to say
so. If it does not, he has just told us the valuation rests on paid traffic alone.

## 4. The counter

He is anchored at 5% total and killed the royalty. You opened at 15% and he called it a non-starter.
The move is not to split the difference on the single number he wants to argue about, it is to put the
royalty back on the table in the shape your halo argument already established.

| Term | Ask | Fallback | Walk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equity | **10% to a Bear vehicle**, 7% guaranteed vesting on time, 3% against an objective metric | 8%, 6 guaranteed | Below 6% guaranteed with no cash |
| Earn-out metric | Blended CAC reduction or total revenue, defined in the agreement | Total revenue only | Anything "at the board's discretion" |
| Royalty | **% of total net revenue**, business-wide, not a Bear range | Bear range plus attribution on Bear-driven traffic | No royalty and no minimum |
| Minimum guarantee | Annual floor in cash, creditable against royalty | Lower floor | All-paper deal |
| Trial window | Mutual or gone. If he can walk at 6 months, so can we, and rights revert | Mutual with rights reversion | One-way in his favour |
| Deliverables | Halve them. 12 posts/stories, 2 shoot days, 1 event. Media mentions best efforts only | 16 and 3 | The current 31 for 3% |
| Whitelisting | Per-post only, campaign-window limited | Per-post, longer window | Account-level |
| Website / social usage | Campaign windows, ends with the deal | Term-limited | Perpetual |
| Anti-dilution | Beckham formula: higher of 1.0% of enlarged shares or a fixed dollar amount over share price | Straight anti-dilution | None |
| Papering | Grant to a vehicle, s.431 election inside 14 days | - | Personal grant with no election |

The reason to reinstate the royalty rather than fight only on percentage: **equity is the part he
controls and the part that can be diluted, delayed and cancelled.** Cash on revenue is the part that
pays whether or not there is ever an exit. He removed it because it is the expensive half for him,
which is exactly why it goes back in.

**Sequencing:** the money and the deliverables are one conversation, and they are traded against each
other deliberately. Give ground on deliverables to hold the percentage, not the reverse.
