## The short version

Battersea is no longer the emergency it was last week. Jesse has dealt with the break notice directly with Christina Weguelin at BPSDC, and as of today that is broadly settled. It is settled in conversation, not on paper, which is the one thing still to close.

That matters because everything else on this page assumed we might lose the room. We probably do not, so we can go back to the actual question: who runs BMF, and as what.

We have two live candidates and they are not competing for the same job. Farren Morgan is reach. Alastair Nicolas is the room. My recommendation is that we stop treating this as a choice and structure it as two roles, because the version where one of them wins is worse than the version where both are in.

## Battersea

The position, briefly, so it is on the record. On 27 July BPSDC wrote to say they want the units adjacent to the gym at Grosvenor Arch for a heritage use under their placemaking strategy. Because the document is a licence and not a lease, they could only recover part of it by terminating all of it, so their route was to serve notice on the mutual one month break and then grant a new licence over the gym area alone. They chased twice, the second time noting they had already begun work next door.

Jesse has since resolved this with Christina directly and the gym is not going anywhere.

Two things I would still do this week. First, get the replacement licence agreed in principle in writing before anything formal is served, because their own correspondence says nothing binds them until a written agreement is executed, and "we would look to grant" is an intention rather than a commitment. Second, push for meaningfully longer than one month's notice on the new one. A one month break is close to no protection at all, and we are about to build plans that sit on top of that room.

There is also a wider conversation on offer about where a larger BMF footprint could work across the estate. That request and their heritage request should sit on the same table so they trade against each other rather than being settled separately.

## Farren Morgan

Farren is the strongest candidate we have had for this, and the situation has a complication in it that is worth naming early rather than late.

He is ex Coldstream Guards, a PTI, nine years served, decorated. He runs The Tactical Athlete coaching people through military, police and fire selection, in around twenty five countries this year. Two hundred and sixty seven thousand on Instagram and he claims a twenty thousand strong email list that fills events of two to five hundred people. Two Guinness World Records set carrying GORUCK packs. For scale, the previous archetype we were considering had about sixty eight thousand followers.

The complication is that he is also taking over as GORUCK's UK Director, which is why you introduced him in the first place. So BMF and GORUCK are one agenda item, not two.

I checked GORUCK rather than take it on trust. Founded 2008 in Florida by Jason McCarthy, genuinely ex Special Forces. Gross revenue reported at forty six million dollars for 2023, so Farren's fifty million was fair. Over ten thousand of their own events run since 2010, so the events capability is real. One caveat held lightly: a third party estimate puts their direct web sales down materially in 2025. Single channel, outside estimate, not a filing. I would not lean on it, but I would ask what UK budget is actually committed rather than possible.

The finding that changes the shape of the deal is an absence. GORUCK's published events calendar is entirely American. A company whose entire brand is events, arriving in Britain with no British events operation. We hold roughly a hundred and forty park relationships, dormant rather than lost, two live sites, and a twenty five year email list. That is what we sell them, and it is worth considerably more than free kit.

Which is the risk. The default outcome here is that BMF becomes GORUCK's UK marketing channel and gets paid in backpacks. Gear at zero cost is a genuine saving and worth taking, but it is not payment for brand access, distribution and park permissions.

Jesse committed on the 27 July call to write Farren a plan and go back and forth by email. That has not gone yet and he has been waiting a week. A draft is ready.

## Alastair Nicolas

They spoke on 21 July. Alastair came back the next day saying he is completely in, that it aligns with his own faith, that he believes remodelling the movement to be Christ centred while integrating fitness is a powerful direction, and he invited Jesse down to see what he runs in Enfield. He has not had a reply since 22 July, which is the same week Farren appeared, and I do not think that is a coincidence.

I would not treat the faith forward angle as the softer of the two ideas. It is the one Bear personally motivated, and Bear's involvement is the main reason the brand still carries value.

## What I would actually do

**Run them as two jobs, not one.** Alastair runs the room: community, membership, the weekly rhythm, the thing that has to be true every Tuesday whether or not there is an event on. Farren runs the reach: mass participation events, national audience, a commercial sponsor attached. A hub and a tour. Held under one brand with a deliberate line between them, that is a better business than either alone. If the line cannot be drawn, weight Bear's motivation.

**Price the GORUCK relationship before anything is announced.** Three things in writing: BMF owns the event brand and its IP, whatever we call it. BMF owns the entrant data and the email list it generates, not the sponsor. GORUCK are a sponsor on a term, with a fee or revenue share and a category exclusivity we grant deliberately. Kit in kind sits on top of that, not instead of it. None of this needs to be adversarial and it is best said as a compliment: worth doing properly because it could be big.

**Answer both men this week.** Farren gets the plan he was promised. Alastair gets a date in the diary at Enfield. Both have been waiting since roughly the same day.

**Get me four numbers.** The size of the BMF email list, which is the single figure that decides whether a relaunch event fills for free. Which park permissions are still live. What the Hyde Park franchise agreement permits. And whether a GORUCK UK entity exists yet or Farren's directorship is still verbal. I could not verify any of them from here and each one is load bearing.
