# Nero x Bear - how the deal works, step by step

**3 August 2026. INTERNAL.** Mechanics only, no reasoning. Full position: `nero-deal-v4-1-2026-07-27`.

---

## Step 1. On signing

Bear is granted **5% of Nero**. Ordinary shares, no conditions, no hurdle, held by a Bear vehicle. He keeps it whatever happens afterwards.

No fee.

## Step 2. The Baseline is fixed, once

**Baseline = Nero's Net Revenue for the twelve months ending on the last full month before the term sheet is signed.**

Tyler warrants that number and evidences it with the Shopify export and the filed accounts. It is fixed on that day and never moves again.

**Net Revenue** is defined once and used on both sides of every calculation: gross sales, less returns, refunds, discounts, chargebacks, shipping charged to the customer, and sales tax. Nothing else comes off.

## Step 3. Every quarter, Bear earns 25% of the growth

Tyler sends a quarterly statement. Anything above the Baseline is growth.

**Bear earns 25% of the growth. Not 25% of revenue.**

If Nero is flat, that quarter earns nothing.

## Step 4. Year one only, there is a $150,000 minimum

Invoiced at $37,500 a quarter. **Fully creditable** against the 25%, so if the growth earns more than $150,000 in year one, the minimum disappears and he just pays the royalty. He only ever pays it if he misses his own forecast.

No minimum in years two and three.

## Step 5. Bear chooses cash or shares

Within **30 days of each quarterly statement**, Bear decides how that quarter's amount is settled: all cash, all shares, or any split.

**Bear's choice alone.** Tyler cannot elect, cannot refuse, cannot require it. It can be 0% shares one quarter and 100% the next. Bear sees the numbers before choosing.

If Tyler will not issue the shares, the amount stays payable in cash.

## Step 6. The price the shares are bought at

This is the bit worth being precise about.

**Price per quarter = the LOWER of these two numbers:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **A. The frozen valuation** | **$12 million.** Tyler's own figure from his opening offer. It never rises. |
| **B. The formula** | **2.0x Nero's verified trailing twelve-month Net Revenue** at that quarter's statement. |

Whichever is lower is the price. The quarter's royalty is divided by that price, and the answer is the percentage of the company Bear receives.

**Worked example.** Baseline $4M. Year one Net Revenue $5M, so growth is $1M and Bear's 25% is $250,000. Trailing revenue is $5M, so the formula gives $10M, which is lower than the frozen $12M. $250,000 divided by $10M is **2.5% of Nero**, added to the 5%.

The same quarter taken in cash would have been $250,000 in the bank instead.

**The freeze is the protection.** If Nero grows fast, 2.0x trailing revenue climbs past $12M, and Bear keeps buying at $12M regardless. Inflating revenue can never make Bear's shares more expensive.

## Step 7. It stops, and there is a hard ceiling

The royalty runs until the **earlier** of:

- **Bear's aggregate holding reaching 15% of the company**, or
- **three years.**

On either, the royalty ends. Nothing continues automatically. After year three Bear has an option, not an obligation, to extend by up to two years. The name licence runs for exactly as long as the money does and stops with it.

So Tyler knows on day one that the maximum he ever gives up is 15%.

## Step 8. What Bear does each year

Two shoot days, six posts on Bear's own channels, whitelisting of approved assets for Nero's paid ads, one in-person appearance, three introductions to Bear's retail and distribution contacts, plus a launch announcement and a quote. Podcast and press mentions are best efforts, never contractual.

## Step 9. What happens if either side fails

**Tyler:** 30 days late on a statement, on allotting elected shares, on any cash payment, or on the annual reconciliation, and the **name licence suspends automatically**. He cannot run a single ad carrying Bear's name until it is cured.

**Bear:** a material failure to deliver in any year **halves** the royalty rate for the next four quarters. A second consecutive year lets Tyler end the deal. A clean year resets it.

**Neither remedy ever claws back the 5% or any shares already converted.**

## Step 10. Protections that sit around it

Anti-dilution floor and pre-emption on any new issue. Tag-along on a sale. If Tyler issues shares to anyone else below Bear's price, Bear's price drops to theirs. On a change of control or an institutional round, Bear elects to accelerate to the 15% cap at the last price or to carry on unaffected. Quarterly accounts, cap table on request, board observer at 10%.

The contracting company must own the trademark, the store and the customer data, and cannot move them out.
