## Three things to settle before this sends

**1. Who it goes to.** The list is 57,620 people and the offer is in dollars. Everything on the pre-order page assumes the US launch. If this goes to the whole list unsegmented we will take UK orders we cannot fill: the UK is a separate print run with a UK responsible-person address, the entity and VAT position is unresolved, and the BG Summit Stack is not legally sellable in Great Britain as formulated, because NMN is not authorised and the trans-resveratrol authorisation covers capsules and tablets only, not a 12g powder. Selling a Summit bundle into the UK is the single sharpest exposure in this whole launch.

Two clean options. Either segment to US subscribers only and send the UK list a separate "you are next, here is the month" email, or send to everyone with a plain line saying founding places are United States only for now and the UK opens later this year. I prefer the segment. A UK subscriber who is told they are next feels looked after; one who reaches the checkout and finds out at the shipping field does not.

**2. The date.** 12 October, as recommended on 27 July. It is the honest expectation plus two weeks. We should ship in September and look good rather than promise September and send a delay notice in launch week.

**3. Bear's voice.** The email is signed from Bear and Jesse and speaks in the family's voice. Bear should read it before it goes, not because anything in it is a stretch but because it is his name on the sign-off.
