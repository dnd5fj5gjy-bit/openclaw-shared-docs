# Defence Partnership Agreement

**Metalectrique aluminium-air technology**

**Date:** [ ] September 2026

**Between:** **Edward Michael Bear Grylls** and **Jesse Grylls** ("**Grylls**"), who may pass this agreement to a company they set up; **MAL Research and Development Limited**, company number 07964862, trading as Metalectrique ("**Metalectrique**"); and **Trevor Jackson** personally.

**This agreement is legally binding on signature.**

---

## 1. What we are agreeing

Grylls will take Metalectrique's aluminium-air technology into **defence and military** markets. Metalectrique keeps the technology and every other market.

## 2. Defence goes through Grylls

**2.1** Metalectrique gives Grylls the **exclusive worldwide right to make and sell products using its technology for defence and military use**. This means anything supplied to armed forces, a ministry of defence, a defence or security agency or a defence contractor, including drones and other unmanned systems.

**2.2** During this agreement Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not sell, license or supply the technology for defence use to anyone else, or help anyone else to.

**2.3** This includes Metalectrique's existing defence contacts: **Saab / BlueBear, Swift Aviation and QinetiQ**. Metalectrique will take those conversations forward through Grylls, and earns the higher royalty at clause 4.2 on any sale to them.

**2.4** Everything outside defence stays with Metalectrique, including cars, civil aviation, off-grid power, consumer products and nuclear decommissioning such as Sellafield.

## 3. Manufacturing

**3.1** Grylls chooses who manufactures defence products: Metalectrique, Grylls itself, or another manufacturer. Grylls can change its choice on 90 days' notice.

**3.2** When Grylls chooses Metalectrique, Metalectrique is paid an agreed manufacturing price on top of its royalty.

**3.3 The electrolyte stays Metalectrique's secret.** Whoever manufactures, Metalectrique supplies its electrolyte at a fair agreed price, and shares whatever else the manufacturer needs, under confidentiality. Within 60 days Metalectrique will lodge the electrolyte formulation with an independent escrow agent. It is released to Grylls only if Metalectrique cannot or does not supply, or stops trading.

## 4. What Metalectrique earns

**4.1** A royalty of **[5]%** of Grylls's net sales of defence products, paid quarterly, whoever manufactures them.

**4.2** A royalty of **[7.5]%** on sales to Saab / BlueBear, Swift Aviation and QinetiQ.

**4.3** The manufacturing price, when Grylls chooses Metalectrique to manufacture.

**4.4** A paid **technical adviser** role for Trevor Jackson on a monthly retainer, plus a day rate for demonstrations and development work.

**4.5** Any price or rate not fixed here will be agreed in good faith within 60 days. If it is not, an independent expert will decide it.

## 5. Contacts and confidentiality

**5.1** Each side keeps the other's information confidential for five years. Metalectrique's trade secrets stay confidential for as long as they remain secret.

**5.2** Contacts Grylls introduces stay with Grylls. For three years Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not deal with them on defence work except through Grylls. If they do, Metalectrique will pay Grylls what Grylls would have earned on that deal.

**5.3** Metalectrique will not use the Grylls name in any material without Grylls's written approval.

## 6. How long it lasts

**6.1** Ten years, then it renews every year automatically. Metalectrique can end it only after a full year in which Grylls did nothing with the technology, meaning no introductions, demonstrations, quotes, trials or sales. Grylls can keep it in force in any quiet year by paying Metalectrique **GBP [10,000]**.

**6.2** Grylls can end it on 30 days' notice.

**6.3** If Metalectrique or its technology is sold, the buyer takes it on with this agreement in place.

## 7. General

**7.1** Metalectrique confirms it has no existing defence agreements that conflict with this one, and that, as far as it knows, it owns the technology.

**7.2** A fuller contract on these terms may follow. Until then, this agreement applies.

**7.3** English law applies. Each side pays its own costs.
