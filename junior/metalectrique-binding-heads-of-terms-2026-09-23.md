## 1. Purpose

Grylls will develop, sell and support defence and military products powered by Metalectrique's aluminium-air technology. Metalectrique will make the cells and power packs and provide the technical support to build them. Grylls owns the customer relationships in defence. Metalectrique owns the technology and keeps every other field.

## 2. Confidentiality

**2.1** Each party will keep confidential all technical, commercial and contact information received from the other, and use it only for the purpose of this agreement, for five years from the date of this agreement. Metalectrique's trade secrets, including its electrolyte formulation and trigger mechanism, stay confidential for as long as they remain trade secrets.

**2.2** This clause replaces any separate non-disclosure agreement between the parties on this subject.

## 3. Non-circumvention and names

**3.1** For three years from the date of this agreement, and for as long as the licence at clause 4 continues if longer, Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not approach, quote to, supply or deal with any person listed at **Annex A** in connection with any defence or military application, except through Grylls or with Grylls's prior written consent. Grylls may add to Annex A by written notice, effective on the date sent.

**3.2** If a defence or military transaction is concluded with a person listed at Annex A outside this agreement, Metalectrique will pay Grylls a sum equal to the gross value of that transaction, which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of Grylls's loss.

**3.3** Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not use the names "Bear Grylls", "Jesse Grylls" or "Grylls", or the name of any company in which either holds an interest, in any marketing, investor, fundraising or press material without Grylls's prior written approval. The fact that Grylls is a party to this agreement does not relax this.

## 4. Exclusive defence licence

**4.1 The licence.** Metalectrique grants Grylls an exclusive, worldwide licence to make, have made, integrate, market, sell and support products using the Technology for **defence and military applications**, with the right to sublicense to a prime contractor or systems integrator.

**4.2 The Technology** means all of Metalectrique's know-how, trade secrets (including the electrolyte formulation and trigger mechanism), processes, designs, drawings, test data and improvements relating to aluminium-air cells and power packs, and any patent or patent application relating to them now or granted later. **Improvements made during the term fall within the licence automatically.**

**4.3 The field.** Defence and military applications means any product or service supplied to or for armed forces, a ministry of defence, a defence or security agency, or a defence prime contractor, including unmanned aerial, ground and maritime systems. Everything else stays with Metalectrique, including automotive, civil aviation, off-grid, consumer and nuclear decommissioning (including Sellafield).

**4.4 Existing defence contacts come inside the licence.** Metalectrique's existing defence contacts, listed at **Annex B** (including Saab / BlueBear, Swift Aviation and QinetiQ), fall inside Grylls's exclusivity. Metalectrique will not progress any defence or military discussion or quotation with them, including its quotation to Swift for a full-size prototype, except through Grylls, and will pass Grylls the details of each. In return Metalectrique receives the higher royalty at clause 5.2 on sales to them.

**4.5 Warranty.** Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson warrant that Metalectrique is not party to any licence, option, exclusivity, letter of intent or grant condition in the defence or military field; that Metalectrique owns the Technology outright, with every employee and contractor who contributed to it having assigned their rights to it; and that no third party has rights that would prevent this licence. They will indemnify Grylls against any loss caused by any of these statements being untrue.

**4.6 Term.** The licence runs for ten years and then continues automatically for successive twelve-month periods without limit. Metalectrique may end it only by giving at least sixty days' notice before the end of a twelve-month period in which **none** of the following happened: an introduction to, or addition of, a person on Annex A; a demonstration, sample, quotation or proposal to a person on Annex A; a trial, evaluation or tender involving the Technology; an order or contract for licensed product; or payment of the continuation fee at clause 4.7. Grylls then has ninety days to do any of these, and if it does, the licence continues as if no notice had been given.

**4.7 Continuation fee.** In any twelve-month period Grylls may keep the licence in force regardless of activity by paying Metalectrique GBP [10,000].

**4.8** Grylls may end this agreement at any time on thirty days' written notice. Metalectrique may not end it for convenience.

## 5. Payment

**5.1 Royalty.** Grylls will pay Metalectrique **[5]% of Grylls's net sales** of licensed product, paid quarterly. No royalty is due on Grylls's integration, installation, service, training or support revenue.

**5.2 Originator royalty.** On net sales to the Annex B contacts the royalty is **[7.5]%**, recognising that Metalectrique introduced them.

**5.3** No equity, shares, options, board seat, or say over Grylls's customers, pricing or business plan.

## 6. Supply, support and security of supply

**6.1 Supply.** Metalectrique will make cells and power packs to a written specification, price list, lead time and monthly capacity, with quality standards and acceptance testing.

**6.2 Technical support.** Metalectrique will provide technical support, development work and demonstrations for a monthly retainer and day rate, with expenses at cost. Within twelve months it will deliver a documented build specification and train Grylls's engineers to an agreed standard.

**6.3 Escrow and step-in.** Within sixty days Metalectrique will deposit a complete technical package (formulations, processes, specifications and drawings) with an agreed escrow agent. If Metalectrique misses an agreed delivery date, fails acceptance testing twice in twelve months, loses a permission it needs, or enters any insolvency process, the package is released and Grylls may have licensed product made by a third party, paying the royalty at clause 5 at half the rate.

**6.4 Terms still to be priced.** The supply prices, retainer, day rate and milestone bonuses will be agreed in good faith within sixty days. If they are not, either party may refer them to an independent expert appointed by the President of the Law Society of England and Wales, whose decision binds both parties. Everything else in this agreement binds immediately and does not wait for them.

## 7. General

**7.1** The parties will sign a long-form licence and supply agreement consistent with this document within ninety days. Until one is signed, and if none is signed, this document governs.

**7.2** Metalectrique will provide, before the long form is signed, a written allocation of export-control responsibility and evidence of product liability insurance naming Grylls as an additional insured.

**7.3** Each party bears its own costs. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction. Clauses 2 and 3 survive any termination.
