# Modern Savage on Wikidata: built, not filed, and why

**1 August 2026. Junior.**

The item is written and ready to run. I have not filed it, because filing it today would almost
certainly get it deleted, and a deleted item is worse for us than no item at all.

---

## What I verified at source today

- **No "Modern Savage" item exists on Wikidata.** Searched, zero hits.
- **Bear Grylls is Q485365.** Solid item.
- **Bear Grylls Ventures already exists as Q26989880**, and it is a bare three-statement stub with
  no description, no sitelinks and no references. It is typed as a *film production company*,
  auto-created from IMDb. Nobody has touched it properly.
- **There is no independent coverage of Modern Savage anywhere.** Four separate searches. Every
  single result is our own: modernsavage.co, Bear's Facebook, Bear's TikTok, the brand Instagram.
  Not one newspaper, trade title, or review site.
- **We have no Wikimedia account.** Nothing in the credential store.

## The blocker

Wikidata accepts an item if it does one of three things: has a Wikipedia article behind it, can be
described using **serious and publicly available references**, or is structurally needed to make
other items work.

We fail the first. We fail the second right now, because "serious references" means independent
ones and we have none. The third is arguable but thin.

Add to that: the Wikimedia Terms of Use make disclosure of connected or paid contribution
**mandatory**. So this cannot be filed quietly. It goes up flagged as coming from the brand, with
only the brand's own website as a source, for a product that has not shipped yet. That is the exact
profile that gets nominated for deletion.

**And this is the bit that matters.** A deletion discussion is a permanent, public, indexed page,
titled with our brand name and the words "promotional" and "no independent sources". Answer engines
read those. We would be creating precisely the kind of source we started this whole exercise to
clean up. The downside is not "we wasted an afternoon", it is "we published a citable negative".

## What clears it

One piece of genuine independent coverage. That is the entire gap. A single trade or national piece
about the launch turns the item from unsourced promotion into a properly referenced entity, and the
batch below runs in about two minutes.

That is the same press push already sitting open from this morning's cover note, which is also the
only thing that touches the January 2026 Forbes profile that Gemini is actually reading. So it is
one job, not two, and Wikidata is a free by-product of it rather than a separate task.

## What is ready

`modern-savage.quickstatements.txt` in this folder. Complete QuickStatements V1 batch, every Q-id
verified live today rather than recalled: label, description, alias, instance of brand, produces
dietary supplements, owned and founded by Bear Grylls, inception 2026, official website, country,
Instagram handle. Each statement carries a reference. There is a marked slot for the independent
source to be added as soon as one exists.

   companies already using the name: Modern Savage Laser Clinic, Modern Savage Industries, and
   Modern Savage Productions. Worth someone checking the trade mark position, if that has not been

2. **Q26989880, Bear Grylls Ventures, is a neglected stub.** Improving it is legitimate data
   cleanup rather than promotion, and it is a much safer first edit than creating a brand item. If
   we ever want a Wikimedia presence with any standing, that is where to start.
