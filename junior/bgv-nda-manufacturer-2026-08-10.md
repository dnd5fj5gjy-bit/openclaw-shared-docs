## 1. Purpose

The Discloser is developing a powdered electrolyte and energy drink product for sale in India in
single-serve sachets. The Discloser wishes to disclose confidential information to the Recipient so
that the Recipient may evaluate, quote for, and if agreed carry out, the contract manufacture,
filling and packing of that product (the **"Purpose"**).

## 2. Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means all information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient,
before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form, relating to the Purpose. It includes without
limitation the product formulation, ingredient specifications and ratios, sourcing, process and
manufacturing parameters, packaging and artwork, sales and volume forecasts, pricing, commercial
terms, distribution plans, customer and supplier information, and the existence and content of
discussions between the Parties.

Information does not need to be marked confidential to be Confidential Information.

## 3. Recipient's obligations

The Recipient shall:

(a) keep the Confidential Information secret and confidential;

(b) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose, and for no other purpose whatsoever;

(c) not disclose any Confidential Information to any person except to those of its officers,
employees and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose, who have been informed of
its confidential nature, and for whose acts and omissions the Recipient remains fully liable;

(d) apply to the Confidential Information at least the same degree of care it applies to its own
confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care;

(e) not copy or reproduce the Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose;

(f) not reverse engineer, analyse or deconstruct any sample, formulation or product supplied by or
on behalf of the Discloser, except as expressly required to perform the Purpose;

(g) not manufacture, or assist any third party to manufacture, any product using or derived from the
Confidential Information for any person other than the Discloser; and

(h) not use the Confidential Information to compete with the Discloser, whether directly or through
any affiliate or client.

## 4. Exclusions

Clause 3 does not apply to information which the Recipient can show by written record:

(a) was lawfully in its possession, free of any obligation of confidence, before disclosure;

(b) is or becomes public knowledge other than through breach of this Agreement;

(c) was lawfully received from a third party entitled to disclose it; or

(d) was independently developed by the Recipient without any use of or reference to the Confidential
Information.

## 5. Required disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation or a competent authority to disclose any Confidential
Information, it may do so, provided it gives the Discloser as much notice as is lawfully practicable
beforehand, discloses only the minimum required, and cooperates with the Discloser in seeking to
limit or resist that disclosure.

## 6. No rights granted

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement grants
the Recipient any licence, right, title or interest in or to any Confidential Information, or in any
intellectual property of the Discloser, whether by implication or otherwise. Any improvement,
modification or development of the formulation arising in connection with the Purpose shall belong to
the Discloser, and the Recipient assigns to the Discloser all rights in it.

## 7. No obligation to proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into any further agreement, to place or
accept any order, or to continue discussions. Any supply arrangement between the Parties will be
governed by a separate written agreement.

## 8. Return and destruction

On the Discloser's written request, and in any event on the conclusion of discussions between the
Parties, the Recipient shall promptly return or irretrievably destroy all Confidential Information
and all copies, and confirm in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain one copy to the
extent required by law or its bona fide document retention policy, which remains subject to this
Agreement.

## 9. No warranty

The Confidential Information is provided as is. The Discloser gives no warranty as to its accuracy or
completeness.

## 10. Duration

This Agreement takes effect on the date above and the obligations in it continue for **five (5)
years** from that date, save that any Confidential Information which is a trade secret shall remain
protected for as long as it remains a trade secret.

## 11. Remedies

The Recipient acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach of this
Agreement, and that the Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific
performance in addition to any other remedy.

## 12. General

(a) **Entire agreement.** This Agreement sets out the entire agreement between the Parties in
relation to its subject matter.

(b) **Variation.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

(c) **No waiver.** No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

(d) **Severance.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in
force.

(e) **Assignment.** The Recipient may not assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations without
the Discloser's prior written consent.

(f) **Third parties.** A person who is not a Party has no rights under the Contracts (Rights of Third
Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.

(g) **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic
signature, each of which is an original and which together form one agreement.

## 13. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, is governed by the
law of England and Wales. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England
and Wales.
