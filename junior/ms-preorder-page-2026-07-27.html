Straight answers

When does it actually arrive?
On or before 12 October. That date has buffer in it deliberately. If we can ship sooner, we will, and you will get tracking the moment it leaves.
What happens if you are late?
We email you before the date with a new one, and you cancel with one reply if you would rather not wait. No form, no chasing, no store credit.
Why only eight hundred?
Because it is a subscription, not a one-off. Eight hundred is the number the first run can keep supplied every month until run two lands. Selling more would mean selling you a second month we cannot ship.
Is the counter real?
Yes. It moves when someone orders and it does not move otherwise.
Can I change the blends in my bundle?
Yes, at any point before despatch. Reply to your confirmation email.
