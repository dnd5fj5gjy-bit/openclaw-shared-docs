Our Honey

Honey in its purest form

Mama Buci produces award-winning, ethical, and socially responsible pure honey made by wild African bees from the Zambian Miombo forests.

The bees forage in the natural local flora, which is what gives our honey its unique flavour. Our honey is pure, cold filtered and free from any additives or preservatives.

Our Story

Supporting over 7,000 families in Zambia

Mama Buci has a unique business model focusing on happy bees, healthy hives and quality pure honey.

Our profits are reinvested back into the local communities. We currently support over 7,000 families in Zambia by providing them with the hives, training and education needed to produce delicious pure honey. Over the years we have watched individuals, communities, and forests flourish.

Honey for Change

Give Hope Africa

In 2005 we founded the charity Give Hope Africa to give children in some of the poorest areas in Africa a chance in life through education. We are currently running projects in both Zambia and Ethiopia.

Our vision is to see every child grow and reach their potential by providing education, promoting community empowerment and increasing self sufficiency.
