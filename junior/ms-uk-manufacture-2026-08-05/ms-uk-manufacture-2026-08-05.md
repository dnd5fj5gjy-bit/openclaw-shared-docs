## The arithmetic, from Sam's own numbers

He gave two: samples within 7 working days of formula and pricing being approved, and 8 to 10 weeks production from PO.

Working forward on the assumption that nothing goes wrong: quotes land this week, you approve pricing on the 10th, samples are with us by the 19th, you approve them inside a week, PO goes out on the 24th. Production is then 8 to 10 weeks, which is 19 October to 2 November. Add inbound to SCEND and pick and pack, and first dispatch is 26 October at the very best and 9 November on the slower end.

That path has no slack in it anywhere. It assumes the quotes arrive within days, that the first sample passes, and that pricing is approved the moment it lands. A failed first sample adds a full cycle and pushes it into late November.

The one lever that genuinely moves this is approving pricing and samples fast. Every day between the quote arriving and the PO going out is a day added to the ship date, one for one.

## The line in Sam's email that matters most

"We'll just need to make sure the animal origin materials are Halal for our certification."

That is one line and it is easy to read past, but it is a condition on the only UK route we have. Halal certification of a finished blend requires the animal-origin inputs to come from halal-slaughtered animals. It is a sourcing constraint, not a paperwork one, and it lands on the organ material, which is most of what makes this product what it is.

It also collides with bison. Rhug in North Wales is our only bison lead and UK bison is very unlikely to be halal slaughtered. If halal applies across the board then the bison line does not get delayed, it dies, and the beef-only fallback becomes the permanent UK formula rather than a temporary one.

There is a way out, and it is in our own thread. When we first spoke to Sam he told us Protein Partners **run segregation procedures**, and that claim is quoted back to him in the 23 July specification email. So the question to put to him in writing, before any PO, is whether a segregated non-halal run is possible for our SKUs. If it is, bison survives and the spec is unchanged. If it is not, we need to know now, because it changes the formula and it forks the UK product from the US one.

That fork is worth naming. If UK material is halal and the US material from ACF is not, the two runs are not the same product, and we are selling one story across both. It also touches the `/ingredients` page, which is the page I flagged for the Ice Miller call this afternoon.
