### 6. Modern Savage product labelling: the website contradicts the manufacturer's spec
**What it is.** On 29 July Junior audited the three live modernsavage.co product pages line by line against Lightning Nutra's final signed-off specification sheets (revision 1, generated 10 July 2026).

**What was found.** The site is wrong and the spec is right, on a long list. Separately, the Summit spec contradicts itself internally: the formula page claims 80mg magnesium and the supplement facts panel on the next page of the same document says 264mg at 63% DV.

**Blocking.** A certificate of analysis or the 21 Nutrient Multi Blend breakdown from Lightning Nutra. Also note that Drew Kouvel warned on 10 July that the auto-generated supplement facts panels in the spec sheets "aren't always accurate" and could be ignored, and Jesse signed those specs on 14 July anyway.

**Jesse decides.** Whether the product pages come down or get corrected before more pre-orders are taken, and who owns getting the CoA out of Lightning.
