## Why it broke, reading the thread back

The gap was there from the start and never closed.

- **7 Aug 10:17.** Farren, on GORUCK funding the whole thing: *"The whole set up? No. Kit, Yes."* And
  then, plainly: *"being honest this feels a little one sided if GORUCK and I do everything?"*
- **7 Aug 10:25.** You set out what you actually wanted: someone who *"sees a value in the BMF brand
  and has an idea of what to do next and we will support them"*, and were explicit that *"what I'm
  conscious I don't want to end up doing is just funding a military games."*
- **7 Aug 10:31.** Farren's position, equally clear: he was bringing the IP, the programming and the
  GORUCK infrastructure, so *"we need to establish a formal joint-venture or equity structure before
  we launch."*
- **11 Aug 17:00.** Josh Williams asked him for a business plan: GORUCK's model, path to revenue with
  BMF, investment required.
- **11 Aug 20:21.** Farren withdrew. **Three hours later.**

Two people wanted the same thing from opposite directions. You wanted an operator who would take
ownership of BMF. He wanted equity in a venture he considered largely his. The request for a formal
business plan was the point at which he decided the structure was never going to land where he needed
it, and he had a ready alternative in GORUCK's own event business.

## What this actually costs

**BMF has no operator again.** Farren was, on our own assessment, the strongest candidate you had had:
267,000 Instagram followers, a claimed 20,000-strong email list that fills 200 to 500 person events,
ex-Coldstream Guards PTI, and he arrived with a funded sponsor attached. Roughly four times the reach
of the previous archetype.

**And the clock is not neutral.** The Battersea gym sits on a licence with a one-month mutual break,
and the position with Christina was described back on 3 August as "kind of solved" in conversation
with nothing in writing. Every BMF plan runs through that room. Losing the operator while the room is
on a one-month break is a worse position than either problem alone.

or bad feeling, and neither is true or useful. He is going to be GORUCK's UK Director regardless, so

the same speed. That question is worth settling on paper before anyone else is approached.
