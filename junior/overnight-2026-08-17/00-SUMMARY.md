# The one thing worth reading if you only read one thing

**Bear has 9,000,000 Instagram followers and Modern Savage is already linked in his bio.
The brand account has 5,167.**

That is a conversion rate of six hundredths of one percent.

Every winner in this category was built on founder-as-face. Heart & Soil went from zero to roughly
$50M in three years, almost entirely on organic content, fronted by a man with 2.8M followers. Bear
has more than three times that reach and none of the baggage.

So the real marketing question for October is not what the brand account should post. It is how
Bear's audience becomes Modern Savage's audience. Everything else in this pack is downstream of that,
and the highest-value asset on the whole content plan is a single "why I made this" piece from Bear,
on Bear's own feed.

One thing to get ahead of rather than be caught by: Bear launched a co-branded "Bear Grylls Survival
Pack" with Ancestral Supplements in 2023, the Liver King brand that later took a $25M
misrepresentation suit. A journalist finds that in ten seconds. The clean and true answer is that he
stopped endorsing and started owning, which is a better story than the question. It should be told
deliberately, once, by him.
