# The two gaps left in Modern Savage marketing

The content plan and the email sequence cover what the brand can do on its own. Two things sit
outside that, and both are bigger than anything in the calendar.

---

## Part one: the ask to Bear

Everything in the marketing plan is downstream of one fact. Bear has 9M followers, the brand has
5,167, and the link between them already exists in his bio. The plan does not work without him.

But "we need more from Bear" is not an ask, it is a complaint. Here is the actual ask, sized so it can
be said yes to in one message, and written so Jesse can send it rather than me.

**Three things, and only one of them takes real effort:**

1. **One piece to camera. The "why I made this."** Ten minutes of his time, once. This is the single
   highest-value asset in the entire launch and nothing else comes close.
2. **Three story frames a week.** The scoop, the morning, no script and no sell. Ninety seconds of his
   day, and it is the cheapest growth lever available to us.
3. **Pin the story piece to his grid.** One tap, permanent, and it catches everyone who arrives from
   the press coverage that is already running.

**Why he should want to, put in his terms rather than ours.** The press is currently writing this
story without him. Hello, AOL, Plant Based News and Spectrum FM have all covered the family's move
away from veganism and Jesse's turnaround in the last few weeks. It is being told about him, secondhand,
by people who were not there. The version where he tells it himself is better, truer, and it is
also the one that answers the Ancestral question before anyone asks it.

### Draft message, for Jesse to send, not me

> Dad, one thing on Modern Savage that matters more than everything else we're doing.
>
> You've got 9 million followers and the brand account has 5,000. The link's already in your bio, so the pipe is there, it's just that nothing's going through it. Every single brand that's won in this category did it on the founder's own feed, and none of them had anything close to your reach.
>
> Three things, and only the first takes any real time.
>
> One, ten minutes to camera on why you made this. The vegan years, Jesse, why you stopped putting your name on other people's products and made your own. The press is telling that story right now without you and getting bits of it wrong. Better it comes from you.
>
> Two, three story frames a week. The scoop, the morning, nothing scripted.
>
> Three, pin the piece to your grid so people arriving from the press coverage land on it.
>
> That's it. Everything else we can handle.

**One note before this goes.** Bear asked on 13 August whether he could edit the handbook before it
went up, and it was already live. He may not love finding that out via a marketing ask. Worth
resolving that first, or at least in the same conversation.

## Part two: TikTok, which does not exist

Instagram is the only social channel linked from the site. No TikTok, no YouTube, no Facebook.

**Why this matters more than a missing checkbox.** This category's discovery has moved to TikTok. The
ancestral and organ-meat conversation is native there in a way it no longer is on Instagram, and the
audience skews exactly where a new entrant needs to reach: people who have not already picked a brand.
Instagram is where you consolidate an audience. TikTok is where you find one.

There is also a structural argument. A launch brand with one channel is one algorithm change from
zero reach, and we are asking people to pay in advance for a product that arrives in October. That is
a lot of trust to run through a single pipe.

**The good news is that we already have the hard part.** Two creative teams are producing video, and
much of the content in the calendar is Reels, which cross-post to TikTok with no extra shoot. The gap
is a channel and an owner, not an asset.

**What I would do, in order:**

1. **Secure the handle before anything else.** Note that `@modernsavage` on Instagram already belongs
   to an unrelated individual, which is why the brand is on `@modernsavage.co`. Do not assume TikTok
   is free. Check it tonight and claim whatever is available, because it costs nothing and gets
   harder every week. This is worth doing before the packaging is printed, since a handle may end up
   on a pouch.
2. **Cross-post the existing Reels.** Zero new production. Start with the three finished assets that
   are already waiting.
3. **Fork the content, do not mirror it.** TikTok rewards a different register: rougher, faster, more
   face-to-camera, less designed. The Jesse piece and the "why organs" education content will travel
   there. The polished product photography will not.
4. **Bear's own TikTok presence is a separate question** and a bigger one. Not for tonight, but worth
   knowing whether it exists and who runs it before building a brand channel that assumes it does.

**What I have not done and why.** I have not checked whether the TikTok handle is available, because
doing so properly means being logged in, and creating or claiming an account for the business is not
something I will do without being asked. It is a two-minute job for whoever owns the brand accounts,
and it is the only step here that is time-sensitive.
