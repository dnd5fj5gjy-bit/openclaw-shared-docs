Two things I noticed on the live site while putting the launch content together. Both sit on pages that are currently taking pre-order money, so I would rather raise them than assume someone has already looked.

The first is pricing. All three products show the same numbers, fifty five pounds a month subscribed and sixty five pounds one off, with the three pouch bundle dropping each to forty six pounds sixty six. That may well be deliberate, and one price across the range is easy to communicate, so I am not calling it an error. But it does mean Mini Savage costs the same as the Summit Stack, and the Summit Stack carries creatine, NMN, PQQ and trans resveratrol while Mini Savage is a smaller pouch for children. One of those two is probably mispriced against its own cost.

Mini Savage is also the one I would be most careful with. No competitor in the category has a kids product at all, so it is our clearest point of difference, and sixty five pounds is a lot to ask a parent for a first try.

Worth adding for context, fifty five pounds across twenty eight servings works out at about two dollars sixty five a serving. Our competitive work in July recommended a band of two dollars ten to two dollars fifty and put the most expensive comparator in the category at two dollars sixty three. So we are currently priced slightly above the top of both. That may be the right call given who is on the pouch, but it looks like a decision worth confirming rather than one that was made.

The second is the line on the homepage that says supporting British farmers. Given the adult blend is made at ACF in Florida, I want to understand what that line refers to before someone outside asks. It may be a specific ingredient, or the plan for a UK production run, or something I am not aware of. I am not suggesting it is wrong. I am saying the family name is on it, the press is actively writing about the brand at the moment, and it is exactly the kind of claim that gets tested.

If you can tell me what sits behind it I will make sure the launch content is consistent with it rather than working around it.
