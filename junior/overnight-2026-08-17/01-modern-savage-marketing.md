## The one number that matters

| | |
|---|---|
| **@beargrylls** | **9,000,000 followers** — and `@modernsavage.co` is already in his bio |
| **@modernsavage.co** | **5,167 followers**, 88 posts |

**0.06%.** Six hundredths of one percent of Bear's audience follows the brand he owns.

The pipe is already built. Bear links the brand from a 9M-follower bio. Almost nothing is going through it.

This matters more than anything else in Modern Savage marketing right now, and here is why. Our own
competitive teardown (15 Jul) established that **every single winner in this category is built on
founder-as-face**: Heart & Soil went 0 to roughly $50M in three years almost entirely on organic
content, fronted by Paul Saladino at about 2.8M followers. Ancestral was built on Liver King. MK
Supplements is one blogger.

**Bear has three times Saladino's reach, and none of the baggage.** That is the single largest
structural advantage any brand in this category has ever had, and right now it is converting at
0.06%.

So the marketing question for the October launch is not "what should the brand account post?"
It is **"how does Bear's audience become Modern Savage's audience?"** The brand grid is downstream
of that.

### One more thing worth knowing

Bear has been in this category before. In 2023 he launched a **"Bear Grylls Survival Pack" with
Ancestral Supplements** ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/trailblazing-global-adventurer-bear-grylls-and-ancestral-supplements-announce-launch-of-bear-grylls-survival-pack-301720110.html)).
Ancestral is the Liver King brand that later took a $25M misleading-representations suit.

That is not a problem, but it is a fact a journalist will find in ten seconds, and it should be got
in front of rather than discovered. The clean line is that Modern Savage is the family's own brand,
made to a standard Bear controls, which is precisely why it exists and why he owns it outright
rather than lending his name to someone else's. **"I stopped endorsing and started owning"** is a
strong, true, defensible story. It should be told deliberately, once, by Bear.

### Three things in that list I would flag before launch

1. **All three products display the same £55 price.** The kids' pouch, the adult pouch and the
   performance stack cannot all genuinely be the same price. Either that is a real pricing decision
   nobody has interrogated, or the shop is showing a template default. Worth ten minutes with the
   Shopify admin because it is on a page that is currently taking money.
2. **Only Instagram is linked.** TikTok is where this category's discovery actually happens now, and
   a launch brand with one channel is one algorithm change from zero reach.
3. **"Supporting British farmers" sits on the homepage** while the product is manufactured in
   Florida and, on our own analysis, legally cannot enter the UK as a US-made meat-derived product
   without a GB-listed producer. Those two facts need to be reconciled by someone before a UK
   customer or journalist does it for us. I am flagging it, not solving it here.

## The plan: three moves, in priority order

### Move 1 — Open the tap from Bear's feed. This is worth more than everything else combined.

At 9M followers, a single well-made Bear post reaching even 2% is 180,000 people. The brand account
in its entirety is 5,167. **One Bear story frame outperforms a month of brand-account posting.**

What that means concretely, and all of it needs Bear's buy-in rather than mine:

- **A "why I made this" hero piece from Bear, on Bear's feed.** Not an ad. The story of the family
  going from vegan to organ meats, Jesse's turnaround, and why he stopped endorsing and started
  owning. This story is already in the press ([Hello](https://www.hellomagazine.com/celebrities/918072/bear-grylls-surprising-confession-three-sons/),
  [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/bear-grylls-used-vegan-now-060000000.html),
  [Plant Based News](https://plantbasednews.org/news/celebrities/bear-grylls-organ-meats-vegan-diet/))
  and it is being told *about* him rather than *by* him. He should own the telling.
- **A recurring story slot.** Bear's morning routine, the scoop in the shot, no hard sell. Three
  frames a week costs him ninety seconds and is the single cheapest growth lever available.
- **A pinned Reel on his grid**, so the 9M who arrive from press hit the story rather than scrolling past.
- **The handbook is the perfect bridge and is being wasted.** It is free, it is genuinely good, and
  it captures email. Bear promoting the handbook rather than the product converts far better,
  because it asks for nothing. Email list beats follower count anyway, since we own it.

### Move 2 — Make the brand account worth following when they arrive

Right now someone landing on 88 posts needs a reason to stay. The content below is built to give one.
The rule throughout: **taste and ritual first, ingredients second.** Our teardown found the entire
incumbent field markets itself as *taste-free*. Nobody owns "the organ blend you actually look
forward to." That is the wedge and every post should serve it.

### Move 3 — Get the earned media working

The family-supplement-venture story is already running in Hello, AOL, Plant Based News and Spectrum
FM. Bear also recorded the Health Optimisation Podcast with Tim Gray on 22 Jul, and there is a clip
of it sitting unposted in the creative team's queue. Free, credible, third-party coverage is being
generated and not amplified. Every one of those is a post.
