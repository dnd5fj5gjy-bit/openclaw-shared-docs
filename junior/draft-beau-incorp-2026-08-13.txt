The bigger point is that this probably unblocks the VAT gate rather than just softening it. Jack's requirement for a VAT number comes from the Fulfilment House Due Diligence Scheme, which applies to goods a fulfilment house stores for an overseas customer. HMRC's own guidance at FHDDS23000 puts goods owned by a UK established company outside the scheme entirely, and a UK company gets the ordinary 90,000 pound registration threshold rather than the nil threshold that applies to an overseas seller.
