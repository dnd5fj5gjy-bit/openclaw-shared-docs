Added. The live list is still sections 3 and 4 of your ops handover, so these have gone in there rather than into a second list nobody reads. Four of the six I can answer now.

1. UK shipping times. Publish 3 to 5 working days. SCEND's default service is Royal Mail Tracked 48 with same day dispatch on anything ordered before 8pm, and Tracked 48 is 2 to 3 working days, so 3 to 5 gives you headroom without overpromising. One thing to hold in mind rather than act on: Jack has still not sent a contract, so those are proposal terms, not a service level we can hold him to yet.

2. US shipping times. Yours to ask Ships A Lot. Worth knowing before you do that there is still no signed contract with them either, so treat whatever they quote as indicative.

3. Barcoding. This is not waiting on the UK company. The licence should be bought from GS1 US in the name of Modern Savage Inc., our Delaware corporation, and GS1's own position is that GTINs are valid worldwide whichever member organisation issued them, so a US prefix covers the three UK products too. The actual blocker is that the purchase has not been made. That sits with Jesse and Raemy and I have flagged it again today. Nothing else on barcodes moves until those ten numbers exist, and the pouch artwork needs them.

4. Branded box. SCEND charge 44p for their standard small box and 72p for a medium, and that is already inside the 5.01 per order. A branded box would be our own supply, so it adds a unit cost, a goods in line and storage. Worth doing eventually, but it is a margin decision rather than an ops one, and I would leave it until the pre-order economics are settled.

5. Handwritten note from Bear and the family. The slot is already paid for, because one insert is included in the 5.01. Genuinely handwritten will not survive the volumes in the plan, so the version that works is a printed card set in his handwriting. Reads the same to a customer, costs nothing on top, and it can go in from the first order rather than waiting.

6. ACF temperature and best before. Both are Drew's to answer and both are worth chasing in the same message as the artwork. Two separate things though. The storage statement, the store below or above line, has to be printed on the pouch, so it is on the same critical path as the barcode. The best before date itself gets applied at fill rather than pre printed, but we need the shelf life from him to know what to apply and to set the reorder cover.

So the three that need someone other than me: the GS1 purchase with Jesse and Raemy, Ships A Lot with you, and temperature and shelf life from Drew.
