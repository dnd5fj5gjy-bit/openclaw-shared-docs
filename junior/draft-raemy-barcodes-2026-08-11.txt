One purchase to make for Modern Savage, and it does not need to wait for the UK company.

Beau and ACF have agreed we should own our own barcodes rather than let the manufacturer buy them for us, which is right. A GS1 licence is a public record, so the prefix has to sit with the brand and not with a contract manufacturer.

The ask is the ten code company prefix from GS1 US, at store.gs1us.org. It is a few hundred dollars plus a small annual fee, and it is cheaper per code than buying the six we need individually. Six get used now, three US products and three UK, and four stay in hand for the next SKUs.

The name on the licence should be Modern Savage Inc., our Delaware corporation, at 303 Peachtree Centre Avenue, Suite 600, Atlanta, GA 30303. You will need the EIN at checkout. This matters because the registered owner is publicly visible and a transfer later is an unnecessary job.

Beau's instinct was to hold this until Modern Savage Limited is registered. It does not need to. GS1's own position is that GTINs are valid worldwide whichever member organisation issued them, so the US prefix covers the UK products too. If a UK retailer ever wants a GS1 UK licence alongside it, that is a few hundred pounds and it is additive.

Timing matters because the barcode has to be on the pouch artwork, ACF's designer is finishing that now, and the pouch is what October hangs on.

SCEND are holding our UK stock arrangement on it, and the FBO registration takes 28 days from the day the company exists.
