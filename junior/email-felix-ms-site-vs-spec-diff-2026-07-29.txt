Following on from the spec sheets I sent earlier. Jesse asked me to go through the ingredient panels on all three live product pages and compare them line by line against those sheets. I have done that. There is a fair bit that does not match.

I pulled the live data from the three product pages on modernsavage.co this morning and checked it against the Lightning Nutra final specs, revision 1, generated 10 July 2026. Where the two disagree, the spec sheet is right and the website is wrong.

Rather than give you a wall of text, here it is by product.


ALL THREE PRODUCTS

Serving sizes and bag weights are all wrong. Every one of them is understated.

Modern Savage: site says 1 scoop (21g), 588g per bag. Spec says 23.5g, fill 657.9g.
Mini Savage: site says 1 scoop (20g), 560g per bag. Spec says 22.0g, fill 616.0g.
BG Summit Stack: site says 1 scoop (10g), 280g per bag. Spec says 12.0g, fill 336.0g.

Servings per container is 28 on all three, which is correct everywhere. The scoop sizes in the spec are 35cc for the two food blends and 15cc for Summit, if you want to update the scoop wording too.

Worth saying, since the UK manufacturing track is not producing yet, there is no separate UK build these smaller numbers could be describing. They are simply out of date.

The salt is named wrong on two of the three pages. Modern Savage and Mini Savage both list "Himalayan Pink Salt". The spec for both is Utah Mineral Sea Salt (fine grain). Summit already says Utah Seabed on the site and is correct, which is how the inconsistency shows up if you read the three pages together.

Honey needs to come off. Both food blends list other ingredients as "Organic Honey Powder or Maple Powder". The formula was locked in late June on maple after Jesse and Bear rejected the alternatives, and the spec says Organic Maple Sugar Powder. Two problems there: honey is not in the product, and an "or" between two sweeteners is not something that should sit on a final ingredient declaration anyway. It should read Organic Maple Sugar Powder, Organic Vanilla Bean Powder.

Bamboo Extract is listed on both food blends and is not in either formula. It only appears in Summit. Please take it off Modern Savage and Mini Savage.

Vitamin D3 is missing from the ingredient item lists on both food blends. It is in both formulas at 8mg of the pine-phytosterol material, declaring as 20mcg. K2 is listed but D3 is not.


MODERN SAVAGE (adult)

Blend totals: the organ blend at 2,500mg, the protein blend at 10,000mg, the prebiotic and probiotic blend at 3,500mg and the superfruit blend at 1,000mg all reconcile to the spec. Those are fine.

The Essential Nutrient Superfood Blend is stated as 1,275mg. The spec components add up to 1,121mg (Aquamin 400, acerola 360, salt 200, 21 Nutrient Multi Blend 123, K2 30, D3 8). Overstated by 154mg.

Vitamin C shows 110mg at 122% DV. The spec label claim is 90mg at 100%.

Vitamin D shows 40mcg at 200% DV. The spec label claim is 20mcg at 100%. That is double, and it is the one I would fix first.

Vitamin K shows 90mcg as "Vitamin K (K1, K2)". The spec has 60mcg of K2 MK-7 and no K1 at all.

Calcium at 120mg / 9% is correct.

The date powder line says it is "the only sweetener", which contradicts the maple sugar in the same panel's other ingredients. One of the two needs to change.


MINI SAVAGE (kids)

The organ and colostrum blend at 1,250mg, the protein blend at 10,000mg and the superfruit blend at 1,000mg all reconcile. Good.

The prebiotic and probiotic blend is stated as 3,500mg. The spec is 3,548mg (inulin 2,381, baobab 1,000, LactoSpore 167). Small, but wrong.

The Essential Nutrient Superfood Blend is stated as 1,240mg. The spec adds to 1,123mg.

Vitamin C shows 110mg at 122%. The spec is 90mg at 100%.

Vitamin D shows 25mcg at 125%. The spec is 20mcg at 100%.

Vitamin K shows 60mcg, which is the right number, but again labelled "K1, K2" when there is no K1.

The milk allergen line and the absence of lung and testes are both correct on this page. Nothing to do there.


BG SUMMIT STACK

The blend groupings all reconcile: creatine 3,000mg, electrolyte and hydration 4,200mg, cellular energy and longevity 1,560mg, focus and absorption 150mg, and D3 20mcg with K2 60mcg. That page is in better shape than the other two.

Two things to fix.

Beetroot and pomegranate are shown at the bottom with no quantities. They are 1,000mg each, which makes them the joint second largest ingredients in the whole stack after the creatine and coconut water. They are currently presented as a garnish. They should be quantified. The spec names them Organic Beet Root Powder and Organic Pomegranate Juice Powder.

Other ingredients on the site read "Organic Rice Hulls" where the spec says Organic NU-FLOW. NU-FLOW is a rice hull concentrate so the site is not misleading anyone, but it should match the spec wording.


TWO THINGS I CANNOT RESOLVE FROM THE PAPERWORK

These are not website errors, they are gaps, and I would rather flag them than have you build panels on them.

Both food blend pages declare a full micronutrient table: vitamin A, E, the B vitamins, folate, biotin, pantothenic acid, iron, magnesium, zinc, selenium, copper, iodine and manganese, each at a percentage DV. The Lightning Nutra supplement facts panel declares only three nutrients against a DV, being vitamin C, vitamin D3 and calcium. Everything else is listed as an ingredient with no daily value established. Those micronutrient figures presumably come from the 21 Nutrient Multi Blend and the organs, but nothing in the spec substantiates them. Before they go back up we need either a certificate of analysis or the multi blend breakdown from Lightning. This matters more than the others because declared nutrient values are the part a regulator actually checks.

The Summit spec contradicts itself on magnesium. The formula page gives a label claim of 80mg. The supplement facts panel on the next page of the same document says 264mg at 63% DV. The site currently says 80mg at 19%. One of the two spec figures is wrong and I do not know which, so I would leave the site as it is until Lightning confirms.


One last general point. The manufacturer panels are US format, percent DV against a 2,000 calorie diet. Useful as the source of the numbers, but not the format for a UK-facing page, so please do not lift the percentage column across as is.
