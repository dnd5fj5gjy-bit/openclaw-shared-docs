## The thing that is now most urgent, and it is not the artwork

**If pre-orders open next week, the page has to be compliant on day one.** In the US the FTC mail-order rule and in the UK the 30-day default mean you must either charge only on despatch, or state a clear shipping window on the page before the customer pays. Getting this wrong on a Bear-fronted launch is a consumer-protection story, not a paperwork problem.

It is not hard. It is a line of copy and a payment setting. But it has to be decided before the page goes live, and the page is next week.

**Second, a cap you set yourself.** You told Phoebe you cannot sell more than roughly 800 subscriptions per territory against an 8-week lead time. That number is in no plan we hold. If the pre-order goes better than expected, you can oversell your own supply chain in a weekend. Decide now whether the page caps founding places, and at what number.
