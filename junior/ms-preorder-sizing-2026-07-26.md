# Modern Savage pre-order: how many places, at what price, and whether the cash works

**26 July 2026. Built because the page may go live next week and three numbers on it are still blank: the cap, the price, and whether you charge now.**

The compliance document covers what the page must say. This one covers what the page must sell. It answers the question the plan left open on Friday: *"Decide now whether the page caps founding places, and at what number."*

---

## The answer, in four lines

1. **Cap it at 800 founding places.** Your own instinct was right, and the arithmetic below says why: it is not a marketing number, it is the largest cohort your first production run can keep supplied until run two lands.
2. **Sell a bundle, not a single bag.** A founding place at $125 raises 2.1x the cash of one at $59.99 and consumes **not one extra unit** of the only SKU you are short of.
3. **At 800 places x $125 the pre-order raises ~$100,000**, against ~$106,000 of committed cash between now and launch. It very nearly funds its own launch.
4. **Charge-now vs charge-on-despatch is not a legal question, it is a treasury question.** One question to Raemy settles it. It is in section 4.

---

## 1. Why 800, and why it is a supply number rather than a marketing one

The thing that makes this different from a normal pre-order: **you are selling a subscription, not a product.** A founding place sold in August is not one unit of demand, it is one unit every month for as long as they stay. The cap therefore has nothing to do with how many bags you can ship on day one. It is about month two and month three.

Run one is 2,500 units per SKU. The Adult blend is the anchor and the binding constraint. So:

| Founding places | Adult units left after first shipment | Months of cover from 2,500 |
|---|---|---|
| 500 | 2,000 | 5.0 |
| **800** | **1,700** | **3.1** |
| 1,000 | 1,500 | 2.5 |
| 1,250 | 1,250 | 2.0 |
| 2,000 | 500 | 1.25 |
| 2,500 | 0 | 1.0 (month two is empty) |

Now set that against the reorder cycle. You told Phoebe eight weeks. Realistically a US reorder is ACF fill plus freight plus receiving, call it eight weeks if pouches are already in stock and ten to twelve if they must be printed first.

**Cover has to be longer than the reorder cycle or you stock out on your own subscribers.**

- Against a 12-week cycle: 2,500 / 3 months = **833 places**.
- Against an 8-week cycle: 2,500 / 2 months = **1,250 places**.
- Allowing for realistic churn (75% month-on-month retention, so three months draws 2.31 x N rather than 3 x N): **1,082 places**.

Every route lands between 830 and 1,250, and the conservative end of that range is 800. That is the number you already named. It is right, and now it has arithmetic behind it rather than instinct, which matters because someone will push you to raise it when the page sells out.

**The honest caveat:** this is the number that avoids a stockout with no heroics. If you are willing to trigger run two *before* the pre-order closes, and accept the capital risk of doing so, you can go higher. That is a real option and it is section 5.

## 2. The bundle is the whole commercial insight

You are not short of stock. You are short of **Adult** stock. Run one gives you 2,500 Mini and 2,500 Summit as well, and neither has a demand engine pointed at it. If the founding offer is a single Adult bag, those 5,000 units sit in a Mississippi warehouse earning nothing while you look for cash to pay ACF.

So sell them in the founding offer. It costs you nothing you need.

| Founding offer | Price | Cash at 800 places | Adult units drawn | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adult only, sub price | $59.99 | $47,992 | 800 | ~57% |
| Adult only, one-time | $79.99 | $63,992 | 800 | ~68% |
| **Two-product bundle** | **$125** | **$100,000** | **800** | **~59%** |
| Three-product bundle | $180 | $144,000 | 800 | ~58% |

Every row draws exactly the same 800 Adult units. The bundle rows raise twice the cash for the same consumption of the scarce thing. Those bundle prices are already your published ones, so this is not a new pricing decision, it is choosing which of your own prices the founding page leads with.

**Recommend: lead with the two-product bundle at $125,** offered as Adult + Summit as the default and Adult + Mini as the family option. Three-product available for anyone who wants it. Not everyone buying has children, so do not make Mini the default and force a no.

**Do not discount it.** The founding benefit is the price lock, the hand-numbered insert and the community access, all of which cost you nothing. Discounting a capped product that is likely to sell out is paying for demand you already have.

## 3. Will 800 actually sell?

Yes, and probably fast. Against the owned list:

| Source | Size | Plausible conversion | Orders |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting list | ~1,800 | 15-25% | 270-450 |
| Founding Tribe | 1,240 | high intent, overlaps above | -- |
| Full email list | 57,620 | 1% | ~576 |

The list alone clears 800 without any paid spend. Treat the cap as a real operational limit that will bind within days, not a scarcity device that may or may not trigger. Which means the sell-out messaging, the run-two waitlist and the "next run ships [date]" copy all have to be built **before** the page opens, not bolted on the morning it sells out.

**On the 12 September stage:** if it sells out in early August, that is five weeks of nothing before Bear is on stage, and the instinct will be that you have wasted the moment. You have not. "Run one sold out in 40 hours, run two opens tonight" is a materially better thing to say from that stage than "please visit our website." Selling out early is the best case, not a problem to be managed. Design the stage moment around run two.

## 4. Charge now or charge on despatch: it is a treasury question

The compliance document recommended charging on despatch, on the grounds that it removes most of the regulatory obligation. That recommendation stands on its own terms. What it flagged but could not price was the cash trade. Here it is.

**Committed cash between now and launch:**

| Item | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| ACF balance, due on ship | ~$80,000 | Aug/Sep |
| Belmark pouches + prepress | ~$13,500 | On PO |
| Ships A Lot onboarding + first month | ~$3,500 | Aug |
| Ku Creatives retainer Jul-Sep | $9,000 | Monthly |
| **Total** | **~$106,000** | |

The timing is the problem. **The ACF balance falls due when the stock ships to you. The customer pays on despatch to them, which is weeks later.** So charging on despatch means the pre-order money arrives *after* the bill it was meant to pay.

That turns a legal question into a single financing question:

> **Can BGV Global carry roughly $80,000 for four to eight weeks?**

- **If yes** (and it has carried $82,000 before, and June speaking income was $220,000): charge on despatch. You get the cleaner compliance posture for nothing but timing, and the pre-order cash then repays the bridge on arrival.
- **If no:** charge now, use the page copy already written, cap at 800, and take the delay-notice obligations deliberately and with the refund path actually working.

One question, to one person, and the decision is made. That is a better use of Raemy than another model.

## 5. Order run-two pouches in the same PO, and the reason has a deadline

Belmark's price breaks are steep and the quote expires **30 July**:

| Pouch | 5,000/SKU | 10,000 | 15,000 | 30,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Large 9x12 | $1.07 | $0.84 | $0.77 | $0.70 |
| Small 7x10 | $0.77 | $0.60 | $0.54 | $0.48 |

Run one at 15,000 pouches costs about $13,500. Doubling that order to 30,000 in the same PO costs about $20,850, against roughly $27,000 to buy the same pouches as two separate orders later, at whatever 2027 pricing turns out to be.

**That is roughly $6,000 saved, and about three weeks removed from the run-two cycle,** because the pouches are already sitting at the copacker when you trigger it. Three weeks off the reorder cycle is what lets you hold 800 places comfortably rather than nervously.

The usual objection is that it commits cash before you know demand, and that a change to artwork costs $150/SKU in prepress again. Neither bites here: the PO cannot be placed until artwork is final anyway, so by definition you are not gambling on a file that might change. The only real cost is cash timing.

**This one has a date on it.** The quote dies Friday 30 July. Ask Sarah for the price hold in the same message as the lead-time question that is already drafted.

## 6. What goes on the page

- **Cap:** 800 founding places, stated as a number and counted down honestly.
- **Offer:** two-product founding bundle at $125, three-product at $180, single Adult available but not the hero.
- **Included at zero cost:** price lock for twelve months, hand-numbered insert card, founding community access from the moment of order.
- **Ship date:** whatever you honestly expect, plus two weeks, per the compliance doc.
- **Sold-out state:** built before launch, not after. Run-two waitlist with a stated ship month.
