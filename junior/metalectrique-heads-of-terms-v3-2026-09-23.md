# Heads of Terms: Defence Exclusivity

**Metalectrique aluminium-air technology**

**Date:** [ ] September 2026

**Between:** Edward Michael Bear Grylls and Jesse Grylls ("**Grylls**"), who may transfer this agreement to any company they choose; MAL Research and Development Limited, company number 07964862, trading as Metalectrique ("**Metalectrique**"); and Trevor Jackson personally.

**These heads of terms are legally binding on signature.**

**1. Defence exclusivity.** The technology means Metalectrique's aluminium-air battery technology, including the electrolyte formulation, the trigger mechanism and the know-how to make products using them. Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson give Grylls the exclusive worldwide right to make, have made and sell products using the technology for defence and military use. Defence and military use means anything supplied to armed forces, a ministry of defence, a defence or security agency or a defence contractor, including drones and other unmanned systems. Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not sell, license or supply the technology for defence use to anyone else, or help anyone else to. Everything outside defence stays with Metalectrique.

**2. Existing contacts.** Metalectrique's currently active defence contacts, Saab / BlueBear, Swift Aviation and QinetiQ, are included in Grylls's exclusivity. Metalectrique will take them forward through Grylls.

**3. Royalty.** Grylls will pay Metalectrique a royalty on its net sales of defence products, paid quarterly: 5% where Metalectrique manufactures the product, 7.5% where it does not, and 10% on sales to the contacts in clause 2 whoever manufactures. The royalty is calculated on the cells and power packs only: where they are sold as part of a larger product, it is calculated on Grylls's price for the power pack on its own. Net sales means the price Grylls invoices less VAT, returns, freight and duties. Nothing is payable by Grylls under these heads of terms until a defence sale is made.

**4. Manufacturing.** Grylls chooses who manufactures defence products and is not obliged to choose Metalectrique, which may quote for any manufacturing. Whoever Grylls chooses, Metalectrique will supply that manufacturer with the electrolyte and any other proprietary component as finished items, at a fair price and in the quantities and timescales needed, and, once the manufacturer has signed a confidentiality agreement with Metalectrique, will give it the information and technical help it needs to make the product to specification. If Grylls chooses Metalectrique, it is paid an agreed manufacturing price as well as the royalty.

**5. Non-circumvention.** During this agreement and for three years after it ends, Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not deal with anyone Grylls has introduced, on defence work, except through Grylls. If they do, Metalectrique will pay Grylls 20% of the value of that deal.

**6. Confidentiality.** Each side keeps the other's information, including contacts introduced, confidential for five years after this agreement ends, and Metalectrique's trade secrets for as long as they remain secret. This replaces any separate non-disclosure agreement.

**7. Names and announcements.** Metalectrique and Trevor Jackson will not use the names or images of Bear Grylls, Jesse Grylls or Bear Grylls Ventures, or announce or refer to this agreement or the relationship, in any material or communication, without Grylls's written approval.

**8. How long it lasts.** The exclusivity starts on the date these heads of terms are signed and runs for at least twelve months, during which Metalectrique may not end it. It then continues year by year. Metalectrique may end it at the end of the first twelve months, or of any later twelve-month period, only if Grylls made no introductions, demonstrations, quotes, trials or sales in that period, by giving written notice within 30 days of that period ending. If it ends, Grylls may keep making and selling products already developed or under contract, paying the royalty, and Metalectrique will keep supplying them under clause 4. Grylls may end it at any time on 30 days' notice. Clauses 5, 6 and 7 survive either way.

**9. Ownership.** Metalectrique confirms that it owns or controls the technology and knows of no claim by anyone else to it.

**10. Next step.** The parties will agree a fuller licence and supply contract on these terms within twelve months of signing. Among other things it will cover the electrolyte price, technical support terms, a sealed description of the process held by a solicitor for release to Grylls only if Metalectrique cannot supply, written agreements with Metalectrique's staff and contractors, export control, insurance, and any quotation already open with the contacts in clause 2. These heads of terms stay in force until it is signed, including if that takes longer, and may be changed by agreement in writing, including by email. They may be signed electronically or by exchanging signed copies by email. English law applies and the English courts have exclusive jurisdiction. Each side pays its own costs.
